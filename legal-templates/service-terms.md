If you are a consumer and we agreed the job at your home, by phone, by email or online, you have the right to cancel within 14 days of the day the contract was made, without giving a reason.

To cancel, tell us clearly by phone, text, email or post using the contact details above. You can use the cancellation form at the end of these terms, but you do not have to.

If you want us to start work during those 14 days, you must ask us to in writing (a text or email is fine) and confirm you understand you will lose the right to cancel once the work is finished. If you then cancel part way through, you must pay for the work done up to the point you cancelled, in proportion to the full price. If we have finished the work within the 14 days at your request, you cannot cancel.

If you cancel and we have already bought materials made to your order that we cannot return or reuse, we may charge you their cost. We will tell you about any such materials before we order them.

Outside the 14-day period, if you cancel a booked visit with less than [CV: cancel_notice_hours] hours' notice, we may charge the reasonable costs we have actually incurred, such as travel or materials already purchased.
