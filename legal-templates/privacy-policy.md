## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit this website, contact us, or use our services; why we collect it; who we share it with; how long we keep it; and your rights. It covers our website, our Google Business Profile, our enquiry forms, and our phone, text, WhatsApp and email contact with you.

## 3. What we collect

**Information you give us**

- Your name, phone number, email address and postal address.
- The address of the property where the work is to be done, if different.
- Details of the work you need, including any photos you send us.
- Anything you tell us during a visit, call or message that we need to do the job.
- Payment details. We do not store card numbers. Card payments are handled by our payment provider.

**Information collected automatically when you use the website**

- Your IP address, the type of device and browser you use, the pages you visit and how long you spend on them, and the website that sent you here.
- Whether you clicked to call us or to send an enquiry.

This information is linked to a random identifier set by a cookie, not to your name, but it is still personal information and we treat it as such.

**Information from other sources**

- If you contact us through Google (a call, message or review on our Google Business Profile), Google gives us your name as shown on your Google account and the content of your message or review.
- If you contact us through Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp, we receive your name as shown on that service and your message.

- Ask us to limit how we use it while a question about it is sorted out.
- Object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests, including for review requests.
- Receive the information you gave us in a format you can take elsewhere.
- Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is our legal basis.

To use any of these rights, contact us at [CV: email] or [CV: phone]. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you have a complaint about how we have used your information, please tell us first. We will acknowledge it within 30 days and try to put things right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
