# Fillout onboarding intake

Clients fill in the Fillout form "Local Launchpad Onboarding". Fillout posts every
submission to Copybot, which keeps the raw answers, builds a client record from the
questions we use, and shows it in the Submissions inbox. Stage 1 (17 Oct 2026) stops
there: no extraction, no page generation from these records yet.

## Setting it up

1. In Railway, set `FILLOUT_WEBHOOK_SECRET` to a long random string (the webhook
   refuses everything until it is set).
2. In Fillout, add a webhook on the form pointing at
   `https://<the app>/api/webhooks/fillout?token=<that secret>`
   (a header works too: `X-Fillout-Secret: <secret>` or `Authorization: Bearer <secret>`).
3. Submit a test form. It appears at `/submissions.html` within a second or two.

## What happens on delivery

`POST /api/webhooks/fillout` (fillout/routes.js, mounted before the login middleware)

- Wrong or missing secret: 401, nothing stored. The comparison is constant time.
- The raw JSON body is saved untouched to `fillout_submissions` and the route answers
  200 at once; processing runs afterwards, so Fillout never times out or retries.
- Same `submission.submissionId` twice: the first row is kept, the second delivery
  gets 200 with `duplicate: true` and nothing else happens.
- A body the parser refuses (bad JSON, no submissionId) is logged and kept as a
  `rejected` row with the text, so nothing is silently lost. Over 2 MB: 413 and logged.
- If processing fails, the raw row stays with `status = error` and the reason; the
  inbox has a Re-run button. Rows left at `received` by a restart are processed at
  startup.

## The client record

- `fillout/field-map.json` says which questions we keep: question id → our key,
  section (the form page), type and niche (`all` or a niche label). Only the
  universal fields and the chosen niche's block are read; every other niche's
  questions are ignored and never stored. **Adding a niche is a config change**:
  add its label under `niches` and its questions under `fields`.
- All 28 niche blocks are mapped (17 Oct 2026), generated by
  `node tools/fillout-niche-blocks.js --write` from `fixtures/fillout-sample.json`:
  it segments the blocks after `q_niche_select` using the 28 voice fields as
  anchors and never types an id. Each block gives `<niche_key>.cat.<slug>` for
  every checkbox group (all in group `services`, so they count once), 
  `<niche_key>.most_wanted_1/2/3`, `<niche_key>.prices_voice`, and optional
  `<niche_key>.other_services` and `<niche_key>.jobs_not_wanted` (Guttering has
  no jobs-not-wanted; Tiling's other-services question is "Additional Services").
  Plumbing & Heating keeps its original un-prefixed keys so existing records
  still work. The niche is matched by exact string equality with the dropdown
  label; anything else gets the "No field map for this niche" banner and the
  universal fields. After a form change, re-run the script with `--write`, read
  its table, then run the fixture.
- Questions are keyed by id only. Names repeat and carry suffixes like "(5)";
  the suffix is stripped for display.
- Values are kept exactly as submitted. Blank = null, "", whitespace, [] (and a
  voice answer with no link and no transcript).
- Each field has a value, a status (`filled` / `blank`) and a source (`form` /
  `transcript` / `manual`). A field typed in on the record page becomes `manual`
  and is never overwritten by a later submission or a re-run.
- Every field has a **tier** (`tier` in the map, mirrored by `optional: true`):
  `needed` (CopyBot can't build a correct page without it: business name, phone,
  email, business address, owner, hours, visit/travel, niche, service ticks,
  most-wanted jobs, prices rows or "not applicable", base town, other towns,
  travel distance, is the quote free and the fee when applicable), `chase`
  (worth chasing: the voice answers, story and team facts, quote process,
  payment terms, insurance, hero and colours, photos received), `optional`
  (never counted; any label containing "(optional)" must be optional, and
  `loadFieldMap` refuses anything else). Two scores: **Ready to build** =
  needed units filled / needed units (the inbox percentage); **Worth chasing** =
  chase units filled / chase units. The old combined score is gone. Menu dots:
  red = a needed unit missing, amber = needed done but chase missing, green =
  both done, grey = only optional. Needs attention is grouped into "Blocks the
  build" and "Worth chasing"; optional blanks are never listed.
- `photos_received` is a manual-only checkbox (no Fillout question, `manual: true`
  in the map, tier chase, Look & Feel) ticked when the photos and logo arrive by
  WhatsApp. It is never written by a submission, so it survives resubmission and
  Reprocess; the form's "I'll WhatsApp them" answer stays visible as information.
- Completeness counts units, not fields, within each tier:
  - a voice answer and its typed twin (same `group` in the map) are one unit,
    filled if either is filled;
  - the five accreditation triples are one optional group;
  - optional-by-design fields (`optional: true` in the map: Town 1 to 3, "anything
    else we should know", "words to avoid", "jobs you do NOT want", "anything else
    that makes you stand out", the "areas, anything else" voice answer, the
    accreditations) are left out of the expected count and shown as "optional
    filled".
  For the sample form (a Plumbing & Heating submission): 69 expected, 10
  optional units, 104 fields.
  - a field with `expectedWhen: {field, in: [...]}` counts only when that other
    field holds one of those values; otherwise it is neither expected nor optional
    and the record shows "Not applicable". Used for the call-out or survey fee
    question, expected only when "Is the quote free?" is "Call-out fee" or
    "Survey fee".
- A map entry may give `position: {after, before}` instead of (or as well as) an
  `id`: the question directly between those two ids in the payload. That is how
  the call-out fee question is found until its id has been seen in a real
  submission (`node tools/fillout-find-question.js q_quote_free eHt6ipCmD9PZXGfmX3wTEZ`
  prints it; then set `id` on the entry). A payload without the question still
  processes; the field is simply blank or not applicable.
- Reprocess on an inbox row rebuilds the record from the stored payload (after a
  field map change, for instance). Hand-typed fields are kept.
- A niche with no field map yet is accepted: the universal fields are stored and
  the inbox and record say "No field map for this niche".

Tables: `fillout_submissions`, `client_fields`, `client_audio`, `client_audio_blobs`,
plus `clients.source / reviewed_at / archived_at` (fillout/service.js).

## Which client a submission belongs to

- A submission is merged into an existing client only when **both** the business
  name and the business email match an earlier processed submission (case and
  whitespace ignored) and that client is not archived.
- Anything less makes a new client. If the name or the email alone matches another
  client (including one created by hand), the inbox row says "Possible duplicate of
  <client>".
- Clients created this way carry a "From Fillout, unreviewed" marker on the Clients
  list and the client page until Mark reviewed is clicked, and can be archived
  (archiving hides the client from the list; the record is kept and can be
  unarchived from the inbox).
- `clients.trade` is set to the Fillout niche label (e.g. "Plumbing & Heating"),
  which is not in the old trade catalogue. The old generation screen takes its trade
  from the uploaded files, not from the client, and the v20 path reports G-NICHE
  (no niche file) rather than failing. Stage 2 decides the niche → trade mapping.

## Audio

Every voice answer is downloaded at receipt and a copy kept, with Fillout's
transcript, link, filename and duration. If a download fails the Fillout link and
transcript are kept, the inbox shows "audio download to retry", and Re-run retries
it. The record page plays our copy from `/api/clients/:id/audio/:audioId`.

**Audio storage.** The bytes live in Postgres (`client_audio_blobs`, base64 text)
behind one interface, save / get / delete, in `fillout/audio-store.js`. Railway's
disk is wiped on deploy and there is no bucket yet, so Postgres is the first home.
About 400 KB per 20 second answer. **Move this to a Railway volume or object storage
before volume grows** (a few hundred clients): write a second store with the same
three methods and swap it in `server.js`. Nothing else needs to change; list and
record queries never touch the bytes table.

## Service prices from the voice answer

After a submission is processed, the niche's "Prices for your services" transcript
goes to the model once, in the background (never holding up intake), and comes
back as price rows: service, from, up to, typical, unit, what moves the price,
quote only, inferred (+ note). Model: `V20_MODEL` through `anthropic-call.js`,
the same as v20 generation; prompt `prompts/Price_Extraction_CORE.md` (only
numbers spoken, never invent or round, spoken forms converted, a range gets
from and up to, a single price gets typical, no usable prices gives no rows).

- The code then traces every number back to the transcript (digits, "k",
  "grand", number words). A number it cannot trace keeps its row but is marked
  **unverified** and flagged; nothing is dropped or fixed silently.
- `inferred_note` and `what_moves_price` are read by a person: the prompt bans
  field names in them, and the record API and page replace any that slip through
  (`from_gbp` → lowest price, `up_to_gbp` → highest price, `typical_gbp` →
  single price).
- Each row is matched, conservatively, to the ticked services and the three
  most-wanted jobs: both sides lower-cased, punctuation dropped, each word cut
  to a simple stem (s, es, ing, ed, er endings off), then equal stems, or one
  side's words all present in the other's ("Patio clean" matches "Patio
  cleaning" and "Patio cleaner", not "Decking cleaning"; a lone word never
  matches a longer name). `matched_to` holds the field key and value, or null.
- Two prices for one service ("£50 per big letter and £20 per small letter")
  become one row: from the lower, up to the higher, the unit, the split in
  what_moves_price, inferred true. The prompt asks the model for this, and the
  code merges any duplicate-service rows the model still returns.
- Storage: `client_price_rows` and `client_price_state` (`fillout/prices.js`).
  State: `pending`, `done`, `extraction_failed` (with the reason and a Retry
  button), `no_transcript`, `not_applicable` (set by the VA). The model gets
  4096 output tokens; a reply cut off at that limit, or one that can't be read
  as rows, fails with "Reply too long or unreadable. Retry, or split the
  recording." A failed run never touches existing rows: transcript rows are
  swapped in one transaction only after a reply has parsed.
- A re-run happens only when the transcript hash changes or on Re-extract. It
  replaces rows with source `transcript` only. Any hand edit, add or delete makes
  a row `manual`, and a deleted transcript row is kept as a hidden tombstone so
  the model cannot bring it back.
- Record page: the "Service prices" card at the top of Services & Locations,
  transcript beside the table, inline edit, Add row, Delete, Re-extract, "Mark
  not applicable". Flags: No prices given, Most-wanted job has no price (one per
  job), Inferred value, a number not traced to the transcript.
- Completeness: `prices` is one expected unit, filled when a row exists or the
  state is not applicable. The count of most-wanted jobs without a price is a
  separate warning (`warnings.mostWantedWithoutPrice`) on the record and inbox,
  not part of the score.
- Tests: `tests/fixtures/fillout-prices.js` with a mocked model; the real API is
  never called in tests.

## Categories and services (the resolver)

- One file per niche in `fillout/services/` (`landscapers-gardeners.json` so far;
  adding a niche = adding a file): categories in order, each with its key, name,
  `tickGroup` (the field map key of its tick box group), `ticks` (what the form
  shows for it, its home services) and `services` in the "All Categories &
  Services" PDF order, with the PDF's duplicate notes kept as `duplicateNotes`;
  `primaryDefault`; `additionalServicesField` (the free-text answer) and
  `additionalTickGroup` (the form's extra tick list, no category of its own).
- `fillout/services/resolver.js` is pure, no AI. A service id is its normalised
  name (lower case, single spaces; no fuzzy matching). A ticked service appears
  as a row on every category that lists it: unique or duplicate (with alsoOn).
  Tabs: `ticked_only` (default; a category needs a tick in its own group) or
  `ticked_or_propagated`. Rows stay in PDF order, interleaved. Legacy keys:
  category_N in tab order, service_N by first appearance walking the tabs in
  order (reused later). Primary = config default unless the client overrides it
  (`clients.primary_category_key`); no tab for it is a flag. Flags: more than 10
  tabs, an unmatched tick, primary missing, and the config checks (a category
  repeating a service, inconsistent casing, a tick box service missing from the
  All list, a duplicate note that disagrees with the lists). `validateConfig`
  reports; nothing is silently fixed. crossRef lists every service on 2+ tabs.
- Additional services (`fillout/additional-services.js`): the ticks in the niche's
  "<Niche> Additional Services" tick group(s) (never unmatched-tick flags) and the
  free-text answer, split one service per line, bullet or comma, tidied, merged
  (ticks first; a service both ticked and typed is one row), deduped against the
  services already in the category lists ("already covered"), and each new one gets a proposed parent from the
  model (`prompts/Additional_Service_Parent.md`: only the client's tabs or
  "unsure", never a new category, never a renamed service, a plain reason).
  Hash-gated on the answer text; a bad reply marks `extraction_failed` for a
  person; hash-gated on the ticks and the text. Rows: proposed / confirmed / rejected / skipped / covered; confirmed or
  manual rows are never overwritten by a re-run; confirmed ones become
  "additional" rows in their parent tab, numbered after the config services.
- Record page, Services panel, "Categories & services": the tabs with unique /
  duplicate rows and legacy keys, the flags, the primary selector, and the
  additional services with Confirm, Change and Skip. API: `GET
  /api/clients/:id/services?rule=`, `PUT …/services/primary`, `POST
  …/services/additional/propose`, `PUT …/services/additional/:rowId`.
  Tests: `tests/fixtures/service-resolver.js` (Edens Edge, both rules).
- The Services panel shows "N of M ticked" for each category's tick group and in
  total (`tickCounts` on the services API). A client whose niche has no services
  file shows "Services not set up for this niche yet" in Categories & services
  and in Pages (Generate page map is off), and a "blocks" item in Needs attention.

### Adding a niche

All 28 niches come from the two "Niche By Niche Categories & Services" PDFs in
`docs/niches-source/` (git-ignored): `node tools/build-niche-services.js` prints the
report (also saved as `docs/niches-source/niche-services-report.md`) and `--write`
writes the files. Never hand-edit a generated file: change the PDF or the
generator's `NICHES` table (PDF title → dropdown label, primary, `alwaysCreate`,
Brandon's declared source errors) and re-run. A niche whose All list holds two
copies of its categories is written to `fillout/services/drafts/` (never loaded)
until Brandon chooses. `alwaysCreate: true` on a category gives it a tab under
`ticked_only` even with nothing ticked in its own group. Two generator rules
(Brandon, 18 Oct 2026): a category whose All list has no services but whose tick
box does takes its tick boxes as its services (`servicesFromTicks`, Blast
Cleaning); an All-list entry named exactly like its own category, with no tick
box, is the GBP default service, kept as `gbpDefaultService` and not as a
service (Drainage).

1. Add `fillout/services/<niche>.json` (copy the landscaper file's shape): the
   categories in GBP order with `tickGroup`, `ticks` (the form's tick boxes) and
   `services` (the All Categories & Services list, in its order) with the PDF's
   `duplicateNotes`; `primaryDefault`; and `additionalTickGroup` if the niche has
   one. If the source PDF itself is wrong (a missing or wrong duplicate note),
   keep the lists right and declare the case in `knownSourceErrors`
   (`{ code, category, service, why }`): it is then reported, not failed.
2. Put the form's checkbox options on each tick group's entry in
   `fillout/field-map.json` (`options`, exactly as the form shows them). They come
   from Fillout itself: `node tools/fetch-fillout-options.js` (needs
   `FILLOUT_API_KEY` and `FILLOUT_FORM_ID` in `.env`) reads the form and reports
   every difference from the niche files, (a) trivial and (b) real, by niche and
   tick group (`docs/niches-source/fillout-options-report.md`); `--preview <file>`
   writes the proposed field map elsewhere for a diff, `--write` records the
   options. All 112 niche tick groups have them (18 Oct 2026). Where the form and
   the PDF disagree and the form wins, set `ticksFromForm` on the niche in the
   generator: its tick boxes (and additional examples) come from the options, and
   form labels missing from the All list are appended (`formOnlyServices`).
3. Run the niche check: `node tests/fixtures/niche-configs.js`. It loops over
   every file in `fillout/services/` and fails on a tick box missing from the All
   list, a tick box with no matching checkbox option (or an option with no tick
   box), a category listing a service twice, an undeclared duplicate note that
   disagrees with the lists, a tick group missing from the field map, and a
   `primaryDefault` that is not a category. Each message names the niche,
   category and service. Logic: `fillout/services/check-niche.js`. Declared source
   errors are typed (a: the note says duplicate of X but X's list lacks the service;
   b: in 2+ categories with no note naming them; c: anything else) and reported in
   those groups; `node tools/source-errors-report.js` prints them by niche and type,
   every (a) in full (`docs/niches-source/source-errors-report.md`).
4. Check against the niche's finished example sheet. Export each category tab
   (and the cross-ref tab, if there is one) as CSV, write the example client's
   ticks as a `.js` or `.json` file (`{ "<tickGroup>": ["Service", …] }`, like
   `tests/fixtures/data/edens-edge-ticks.js`), then run:

   ```
   node tools/check-against-sheet.js <niche> --ticks <ticks file> --rule ticked_or_propagated <csv files...>
   ```

   It is read-only. Content differences (a tab, service, key, unique/duplicate
   or "also on" note) are listed apart from order differences (same rows, other
   position); exit code 1 when content differs. Use `--rule ticked_only` too if
   the sheet left out categories the client never ticked. Edens Edge today: every
   key matches; 5 content differences in the notes and Hardscaping on Paving
   Contractor, and 2 order differences (PDF order vs the sheet's).

## The Clients landing page: niche tabs and allocation

- Niches come from `fillout/field-map.json` through `fillout/niches.js`: 28 entries,
  each with a stable key (`window-cleaning`) and the Fillout label as display name,
  plus `other` ("Other / unsorted"). `nicheKeyFor(trade)` maps a Fillout label or an
  old trade catalogue name ("Roofing Company", "Roofer", "Landscaper Gardener") to a
  key; Carpenter, Plasterer, Skip Hire and anything unknown go to `other`.
- `clients` has `niche_key`, `signed_on` (date), `allocated_at`, `allocation_source`
  (`auto` from the migration, `confirmed` from New Clients, `manual` from the New
  Client form or Move to another niche). No `allocated_at` = new, waiting in New
  Clients. `fillout/allocation.js` adds the columns and runs the idempotent
  migration at startup: hand-made clients (and archived Fillout ones) are placed
  by their trade with `signed_on = created date`; live Fillout clients are left
  for New Clients. A resubmission never touches these columns.
- `/clients.html`: search (name and email, across all niches), Show archived,
  a scrolling tab bar (`?tab=key`, remembered in localStorage; lands on New
  Clients when it has any): New Clients (n), one tab per niche with a client
  (alphabetical, with counts), Other / unsorted, All clients. Counts exclude
  archived. Niche tabs list the usual rows plus the signed date and a Move to
  another niche dropdown. New Clients shows each unallocated client with its
  badges, the form date, the suggested niche from the Fillout answer and the
  question "Is this client a …?" with Yes, a dropdown and an editable date;
  allocating saves the four columns (`confirmed`) and moves the client without a
  reload. No suggestion → "No suggestion, pick a niche", Allocate disabled until
  one is chosen. New Client asks for niche and signed date and allocates at once
  (`manual`).
- API: `GET /api/clients` (now also `tabs` and `niches`, and per client
  `niche_key`, `niche_label`, `signed_on`, `allocated`, `allocation_source`,
  `submitted_at`, `fillout_niche`, `suggested_niche_key`, `duplicate_of`),
  `GET /api/niches`, `POST /api/clients` (`name`, `niche_key`, `signed_on`; old
  callers sending `trade` still work), `POST /api/clients/:id/allocate`,
  `POST /api/clients/:id/move`. Tests: `tests/fixtures/clients-niches.js`.

## Pages and API

- `/submissions.html`: every submission, newest first: business name, niche,
  received, status (received / processed / error / rejected), completeness, flags.
  Click a row for the record. `GET /api/fillout/submissions`,
  `POST /api/fillout/submissions/:id/reprocess`.
- `/client-record.html?id=<clientId>`: one panel at a time. Left menu (a dropdown
  on narrow screens) with "filled of expected" counts and a dot per panel: red
  when an expected answer is missing, green when all are filled, grey when the
  panel has nothing expected. Panels come from `panels` in
  `fillout/field-map.json` and each field's `panel` (its `section` is still the
  form page and drives the "Original answers" view at the bottom of the menu):
  Business Information, About Business, Target Audience, Areas & Local Details |
  Services, Prices, Quotes & Process | Voice & Tone, Look & Feel | Credentials,
  Reviews & Social. Each panel carries a Required / Recommended / Optional tag and
  a line on what it is used for. Deep links by hash (`#services`,
  `#services:landscapers.cat.gardener`). Fields: label over value, help line
  where the map has one, source tag (from form / from recording / edited by
  hand), click to edit with autosave and a "Saved" note by the header. Blank
  expected fields stay visible; blank optional fields collapse into one line per
  panel. Voice + typed pairs are one card (player, transcript toggle, typed twin
  beneath); service ticks are chips; the five accreditations are one table.
  Overview (the landing page) shows the score, counts, submission, a duplicate
  warning, and the "Needs attention" list from `fillout/attention.js`
  (`attention` on the record API): blank expected answers by panel, one "No
  prices given" or one line per unpriced most-wanted job, inferred or unverified
  price rows, a failed extraction with Retry, failed audio copies, no field map,
  an errored submission; empty means "Ready to build". Light and dark themes via
  CSS variables, system default, the choice kept in localStorage when allowed.
  The record API also returns `panels` (counts by the same unit rules as the
  headline, so they add up) and `duplicateOf`; nothing else about it changed. `GET /api/clients/:id/record`,
  `PUT /api/clients/:id/record/:key` `{value}`, `GET /api/clients/:id/audio/:audioId`,
  `POST /api/clients/:id/archive | unarchive | reviewed`.
