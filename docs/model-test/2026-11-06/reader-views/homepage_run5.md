## Your Landscaping Questions Answered

### How much does landscaping cost in Southampton?

The biggest factor is how much groundwork the job needs. A small border costs far less than a patio. Digging out, clearing soil and preparing the ground is where most of the hours go, and materials then move the figure again. Send photos and the rough size and we'll give you a real price.

### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

A reasonable quote ties each cost to something the job involves. For a patio, that means the area covered, how much soil is taken away and the type of paving chosen. A large garden with awkward access costs more than a small, open one, and the quote should show why.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually the water has nowhere to go. Around Southampton, the ground under a patio or lawn may drain slowly, especially on low land near the rivers. A surface that slopes the wrong way adds to it, and so does having no drain or soakaway to take the water. Finding which cause it is comes first, then the fix.
