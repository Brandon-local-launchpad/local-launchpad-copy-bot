## What's Included in a Lawn Mowing Visit

Lawn mowing is a cut for homeowners who'd rather spend the weekend on something else. A visit covers the lawn from the first walk over the grass to the last of the edging, with the clippings dealt with as agreed.

- **Inspect lawn and remove debris**: Walk the lawn, pick up stones and sticks
- **Set cutting height**: Cutting height checked against the grass length
- **Mow**: Rotary or cylinder mower, in even passes
- **Edge or strim**: Edges along paths and beds cut back to the line
- **Collect or disperse clippings**: Clippings boxed up or mulched back in
- **Clear paths**: Paths and hard edges cleared of grass

Cutting too much at once is where lawns come off worst. Taking off far more than about a third of the blade length tears the grass instead of slicing it. Running the blades low to save time scalps the high spots in the same way. A lawn cut like that turns pale and patchy, and it can take weeks to knit back together.

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Long grass and edges lapping over the path are the signs. Between spring and midsummer, once the soil has warmed, growth is at its fastest, and a missed visit shows quickly. Grass left that long is cut unevenly or scalped when it's finally tackled. A visit sets the cutting height, mows and collects the clippings.

### It looks untidy from the house

Grass over the path edge, a ragged line against the bed and clumps on the lawn are what show from the door. Between cuts the edges creep outward, fastest in late spring when the lawn is growing hardest. The visit cuts each edge back to its line and collects the clippings or spreads them as agreed.

### It keeps coming back

Some return is normal, like moss after a wet winter where the ground stays damp, or a flush of growth after warm rain. A weed that comes straight back after a treatment in the growing season isn't, because the cause was never dealt with, only what showed. Treatment belongs in spring or autumn, while the plant is growing.

### What the season is about to throw at it

Spring growth comes all at once as the soil warms, and that's the season doing what it does, not a fault. Getting ahead of it means each cut only takes the top off the grass. The edges get trimmed back at the same time, so nothing is cut down from a long stretch in one go.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

How often depends on how fast the grass is growing. Warm, damp spells speed it up, and a dry spell slows it right down. In a long drought, a lawn is better left uncut than stressed. The pattern of visits is agreed with you, and nothing is fixed to the calendar.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

The length and dampness of the grass moves the price most. Longer growth takes more cutting and clearing, so the figure follows the lawn itself. Quotes are free, and a few photos of the garden give us enough to put a figure in front of you.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

We're insured up to £5m for the work we do. That cover is there in case something goes wrong with the work itself during a lawn mowing visit, such as damage to a lawn, edge or path while we're cutting. It's described here rather than promised, so ask if you'd like the details first.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

Not necessarily, it depends on how the garden is reached. Side gates, keys and whether you'd like to be there are all agreed before the first visit. A locked gate is worth mentioning early. So is a lawn at the back with no side access.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit covers the cut and the tidying around it. Debris is picked up, the height is set for the grass, the lawn is mowed, and the edges are trimmed where the mower can't reach. Clippings are then collected or mulched back in. Hedges, weed treatment and mulching aren't part of a mowing visit, but [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) can be added alongside it.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

There's no single rulebook for lawn mowing, and nothing to apply for. Grounds maintenance guidance is usually split into parts. They cover lawns, hard areas, planting and water, and the part that fits the job is the one followed. It describes how the work is usually done, so each lawn is judged on its own.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

Once the grass has started to grow, which usually follows the soil warming in spring. At that point the lawn has enough growth to need cutting, so the start follows the weather rather than the calendar. A cut before then has very little to take off, and the first one of the year only needs to take the top off.
