## What's Included in a Lawn Mowing Visit

Lawn mowing is the regular cut for a lawn that needs keeping in shape, with the edges and the paths finished at the same visit. Each visit leaves the lawn at the height set for it and the cuttings dealt with.

- **Inspect lawn and remove debris**: Lawn checked and debris picked up before cutting
- **Set cutting height**: Mower height set for the length of the grass
- **Mow**: Lawn cut in passes across the whole area
- **Edge or strim**: Lawn edges cut back along paths and borders
- **Collect or disperse clippings**: Cuttings collected or spread back over the grass
- **Clear paths**: Paths swept clear of grass and cuttings

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

RETURN: card_body: Between spring and midsummer, grass grows fastest, and a lawn left without a visit gets long quickly. How quickly depends on the weather, how hard the grass is growing and how long it has gone since the last cut. Long grass cut back in one go gets scalped, and the clumps left behind shade the grass underneath.

### It looks untidy from the house

Grass creeping over a path, a ragged line against the border and cuttings left in lumps all come from edge growth pushing out between cuts. It's fastest in late spring, when the lawn is growing hardest, and the visit puts the edge back on its line and clears the lumps. The edge starts creeping again as the grass grows.

### It keeps coming back

RETURN: card_body: Moss after a wet winter, and a flush of growth after warm rain, are normal. A weed back in the growing season, soon after it was cleared, is not. Mowing only removes what shows above the grass, and the plant beneath carries on. [Weed control](/services/weed-control) treats the plant itself while it's growing, in spring or autumn.

### What the season is about to throw at it

Spring growth arrives all at once as the soil warms, and that flush is the season doing what it does, not a fault. Getting ahead of it means a first cut before the grass outruns the mower. After that, each cut takes off only a little, because taking too much at once stresses the lawn.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

What decides it is how fast the grass is growing, which the weather sets. Cuts usually run from March to October, and they pause in a long drought or on ground that's wet or frozen. A quiet spell stretches the gaps, while a wet spring pulls them closer. The schedule is agreed with you before the first visit.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

How much grass there is to cut is the biggest factor in the figure. Quotes are free, so ask for one and we'll give you a number before anything is cut. The full breakdown is in How We Price Lawn Mowing Visits in Southampton.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit covers the cut itself, the edges, the paths and the cuttings. The lawn is walked for debris first, then the whole area is cut. Work beyond the lawn, such as boundary repairs, is a separate job, and [fencing](/services/fencing) is one example.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

Yes, in most cases. Grounds maintenance guidance usually applies to lawn work, and it describes how the job should be done rather than how often it's needed. You don't have to apply for anything or fill in any forms.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first cut goes in once the grass starts growing again. A cut does little for grass that isn't growing, so the timing follows the growth. Dates change from year to year with the weather, so the first visit is set when the lawn is ready.
