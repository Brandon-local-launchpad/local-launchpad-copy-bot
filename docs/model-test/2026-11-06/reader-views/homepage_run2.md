### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

A reasonable quote spells out what you're paying for. One that says only patio beside a single total leaves you guessing. A quote that names the surface, the area and whether the old surface is taken away answers most questions before you ask.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually it's one of three causes, and the fix depends on which. The ground may drain slowly, the surface may slope the wrong way, or there may be no drain or soakaway to take the water. Near the rivers and the coast, Southampton's ground can hold water close to the surface in wet months. Regrading the surface or adding a drain or soakaway are the usual fixes.
