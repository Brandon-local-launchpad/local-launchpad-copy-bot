### How much does landscaping cost in Southampton?

### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

It's reasonable when it describes the job you asked for in enough detail to judge. A quote that gives the size, the materials and the finish is easier to check than a single total. If something isn't listed, ask whether it's included before you agree.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually the ground can't take the water away, or the surface falls the wrong way. Common causes are slow draining ground, a surface that falls towards a low spot, and no drain or soakaway to carry the water off. Some sites near the coast and on reclaimed land are slow to drain. A test hole dug where the water sits shows which cause is at work.
