## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

A visit missed in spring or summer gets away from you fast, as growth peaks then. The weather that week decides how fast it grows, so no fixed gap suits every lawn. By the next visit it has grown past where it was left. That visit has to bring it back under control, not simply keep it there.

### It looks untidy from the house

From the kitchen window, grass creeping over the path is the first thing you notice. The lawn edge looks ragged where it meets the beds. Late spring is when it moves fastest. Between cuts it creeps back out, so the edge needs trimming to its line every visit.

### It keeps coming back

A flush of weeds after warm rain is normal. What isn't normal is the same weed coming back soon after clearing. How quickly it returns depends on the plant's growth, the season and the treatment used. Treatments timed for when the plant is growing, in spring or autumn, aim at what's behind the regrowth, though a return can still happen.

### What the season is about to throw at it

A flush of growth in spring is the season doing what it does, not a fault. Once the soil warms, the grass can put on a lot in a short time. Taking more than a third of its height off in one cut stresses it. So getting ahead of it means cutting often enough that each cut stays light.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on how fast the grass is growing. That changes with the weather, so a fixed number of cuts a month doesn't fit every lawn. Cutting is held off in dry spells and on ground that's waterlogged or frozen. The schedule is agreed with you.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m. That cover is for accidental damage caused by mowing, such as a stone flicked from the blade into a greenhouse. Damage usually happens where the grass runs right up to a fence, a wall or a parked car. Those are the spots worth pointing out before the first visit.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

Whether you need to be home comes down to how the mower gets to the lawn. A side gate wide enough for it, or a route through the house, is what decides it. Locked gates and pets are worth mentioning before the day, so the visit isn't held up. Let us know what applies to your garden and we'll go from there.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit checks the grass, cuts the whole lawn and tidies up after. Edges, cuttings and paths come last. Hedge trimming isn't part of it, but it can be added to the same visit as an extra, see [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming).

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

No single rule sets how often a lawn is cut. Lawn work is usually done to a recognised standard, which treats amenity grass, hard areas and soft landscape as separate jobs. Nothing needs applying for on your side.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first cut goes in once spring growth has started. Before then there's little to cut, and a winter visit would leave the mower idle. If you also want [mulching](/services/mulching), raise it at the same time, while the beds are still open.
