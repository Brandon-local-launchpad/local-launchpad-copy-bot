## What's Included in a Lawn Mowing Visit

A lawn mowing visit is a cut, an edge and a clear up for lawns that have got longer than their owners would like. It's for homeowners who want the grass back to an even, tidy height with the borders neatly trimmed.

- **Inspect lawn and remove debris**: Walk the lawn and pick up sticks and stones
- **Set cutting height**: Cutting height set for the grass on the day
- **Mow**: Lawn cut with a rotary or cylinder mower
- **Edge or strim**: Lawn edges trimmed back where grass meets paths and beds
- **Collect or disperse clippings**: Clippings collected or mulched back into the lawn
- **Clear paths**: Paths swept clear of cuttings

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Long grass, edges lapping over the path and clumps of cuttings are the signs a visit was missed. From spring to midsummer, once the soil has warmed, growth is at its fastest. Mowing then clears the clippings, because long grass cut hard gets scalped and the clumps shade what's underneath.

### It looks untidy from the house

From the house, the edges give it away: grass over the lip of the path and a ragged line where the lawn meets the bed. Between cuts, edge growth creeps over paths and beds, fastest in late spring. A visit trims it back to its line and collects the clippings or spreads them on the lawn.

### It keeps coming back

Some return is normal: moss after a wet winter where the ground stays damp, or a flush of growth after warm rain. A weed that comes straight back after a tidy up is different, because only what shows was dealt with. The cause is treated while the plant is growing, which is what [weed control](/services/weed-control) is for.

### What the season is about to throw at it

Once the soil warms in spring, grass puts on its growth all at once. That's the season doing what it does, not a fault in the lawn. The first cuts are easiest while the grass is still short. Once a flush of growth sets in, each cut gets harder.

## How We Price Lawn Mowing Visits in Southampton

How much grass a visit has to get through moves the price more than anything else. Once you've asked for a quote, the price is agreed with you before any work starts.

### What a visit has to get through

Long, wet or thick grass is slower going. The mower clogs, it needs extra passes to cut evenly, and heavy clippings take longer to collect. Dry, short grass is quick work, so the same lawn can be priced differently from one quote to the next.

### How much is in the way

Trees, play equipment, beds and slopes all mean the mower has to stop, turn or be steered round something. A narrow gate slows getting it to the lawn. Each adds time to a visit, so a lawn with plenty in the way costs more.

### The first visit

A lawn left uncut for a whole season makes the first visit a bigger job, with long growth to bring down to a workable height. After that, each visit is priced as a visit, so the first one usually costs more.

### Extras you add to the visit

Work beyond the cut can go on the same visit, such as hedge trimming, weed control or mulching the beds. Each one is its own job with its own time and materials. That's why a lawn with a hedge and beds to do costs more than the mowing alone.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on the season and the weather, and the pattern is agreed with you. Mowing runs from March to October, while growth is under way. It eases off in a prolonged summer drought and stops on very wet or frozen ground, where cutting does more harm than good.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

Size of the lawn moves the price most. Free quotes are how you get a figure, and photos of the lawn, its gates and its beds help us price it.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m. The cover is for the work we carry out on a visit. That means the cutting and edging, and the clearing up afterwards. It applies in the same way to our other garden work.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

Arrangements for getting in are agreed before the first visit. That's the point to say how the lawn is reached and what suits you, so it's all settled before anyone turns up.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit covers a debris check, the cut and the edges. The cutting height is set for the grass on the day. Clippings are then collected or left to mulch back in. Hedges and weeds aren't part of a mowing visit, but [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) can be added alongside it when you want the hedge done as well.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

There are standards, but there's nothing for you to apply for. Industry standards cover looking after grass, hard areas and planting, with a separate part for each kind of ground. They usually guide how the work is done. None sets one pattern that suits every lawn, because that depends on the grass and the garden.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first cut goes in once the grass has started growing again. That's usually in spring, when the ground has warmed enough for the roots to get going and there's new growth to cut. Cutting before then does little for the lawn.
