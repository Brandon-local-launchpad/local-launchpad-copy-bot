### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

Look for a quote that itemises the work and the materials. A single total gives you nothing to check the price against. Ask what the figure covers, what it leaves out, and whether removing the old surface is included. Clear answers to those make the price easy to judge.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Water usually pools because it has no way out. Clay or dense soil can hold water instead of draining it. A surface that slopes the wrong way lets runoff collect in one spot. Without a drain or soakaway, the water stays put. Near the rivers and coast, the ground is often wet already. A new fall can be cheap, but a drain means digging, so test the wettest spot first.
