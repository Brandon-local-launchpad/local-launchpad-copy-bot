### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

It comes down to whether the quote describes the actual work. Each part of the job should be named, such as the area being paved and the depth dug out. The base laid and the finish on top should be named too. A single total for "a patio" leaves you unable to tell what you're paying for.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually it's slow draining soil, a surface that falls the wrong way, or no drain. Soil in Southampton isn't the same everywhere, and some of it stays soggy through winter, especially near the rivers and the coast. A new fall on the surface, a land drain or a soakaway can each fix a different cause. [Landscape gardener](/landscape-gardener/) work covers that kind of reshaping.
