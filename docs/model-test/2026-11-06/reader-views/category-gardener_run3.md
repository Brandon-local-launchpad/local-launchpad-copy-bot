## Common Gardening Problems

### A lawn full of moss and bare patches

Moss in a lawn after a wet winter is normal, and it thins as the grass grows in spring. What isn't normal is moss that holds on through summer, or bare patches that never fill. Shade and damp ground keep it there. Scarifying lifts it, and overseeding the thin patches in spring or early autumn fixes them.

### Hedges that have got away

RETURN: card_body: Growth left through spring and summer goes woody from the inside out, and cutting into that old wood leaves gaps that won't fill. A cut before midsummer, while new growth is still soft, keeps the blades in young shoots. The right window depends on the species. Bringing a neglected hedge back takes cuts in stages over several seasons.

### Weeds that come straight back

Weeds that reappear soon after clearing have usually had their roots left in the ground, and a mown or snapped off top just regrows. Hoeing and lifting them in spring, while they're small and before the roots go deep, cuts down how fast they return. No method keeps them away for good.

### Leaves and debris burying the lawn

Leaves falling in autumn are normal, not a fault. The trouble starts where they lie, because they keep light and air off the grass underneath. How fast it shows depends on how thick the fall is and how wet the weather stays. Clearing them through autumn, before they mat down, gives the grass a better chance through winter.

## What Affects the Cost of Gardening in Southampton

The biggest factor in the price is how much garden there is to look after on each visit. A rough idea of the lawn, beds and hedges is enough to start from.

### How often we visit

RETURN: card_body: Visits on a regular round are shorter, because the work is caught up from one visit to the next. A single big tidy up has a season's growth to clear in one go, so it takes longer and costs more.

### The size of the garden

How big the garden is decides how long a visit runs. Each extra patch of lawn, border or hedge adds time to the round. Plenty of small gardens, with one hedge and a strip of grass, take much less.

### What each visit covers

A visit covering the whole garden is more than one cut, and the price follows the list. The round takes in the lawn and edges, weeds lifted from the beds, and any hedge that's due. Leave a part off and the figure drops.

### Green waste

Whether the cuttings, prunings and leaves are taken away or left on site changes the job, and that choice moves the price. Mention it when you ask for your quote, so the figure reflects it.
