## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Long grass and beds running over their edges usually mean a visit was missed in spring or early summer. How fast it gets away depends on the weather and time since the last cut. Left that long, the next cut takes off more than the grass can spare. Visits keep each cut to about a third of the height.

### It looks untidy from the house

From a window, the first thing to show is grass over the path or a ragged edge against the beds. Between cuts, edge growth creeps over paths and into beds, fastest in late spring when the lawn is growing hardest. A visit cuts the edge back and clears the cuttings, but the creep starts again after that.

### It keeps coming back

Moss after a wet winter, or a flush of growth after warm rain, is the lawn doing its job, so a cut is enough. A weed that keeps returning after treatment still has its cause in place, and only the top has been dealt with. Treatment timed for active growth, in spring or autumn, is when it takes hold.

### What the season is about to throw at it

The spring flush is the season at work, not a fault. As the soil warms, growth picks up across the lawn at once. How fast it comes depends on the weather, the feeding and last year's cutting. Getting ahead of it starts with the blade set before the surge, so each cut takes only a little.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on how fast the grass is growing. The weather and the grass type set that pace. Growth runs from March to October, speeding up in warm, wet weather. It slows in a dry spell, or on ground that's wet or frozen. So we agree the pattern with you, rather than working to a fixed calendar.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

The amount of grass to cut moves it most. A lawn left to grow long takes more work than one kept short. Ask for a free quote with the rough area and what needs doing, and you'll have a figure before any work starts.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m for the work we do at your home. The cover is there for that work, which is the part of the job that matters if something goes wrong on the day.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

The arrangements for getting in are agreed with you in advance. If there's a side gate, a locked area or a dog, mention it when you first enquire, so it's planned in.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A lawn mowing visit covers the cut and the tidy up around it. Beyond the cut itself, we trim the edges, deal with the clippings and leave the paths clear. Hedge trimming isn't part of the mow, so if the hedges need it, [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) can go on the same visit.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

Nothing needs applying for on your side. The work usually follows a grounds maintenance standard, which sets out different approaches for lawns, hard ground and water areas. In most cases that means each area is handled on its own terms, not under one routine for the whole garden.
