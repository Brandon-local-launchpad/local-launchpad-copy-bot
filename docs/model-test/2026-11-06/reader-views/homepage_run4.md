Borders that look tidy in May and tangled by August usually mean the garden has been left too long. Our [gardener](/gardener) work covers fencing, hedge trimming and weed control, so the garden stays in order through the year.

### [Landscape Gardener](/landscape-gardener)

Is a patchy, tired lawn something a [landscape gardener](/landscape-gardener) visit can fix, or just a bad season? Usually it's lawn care and garden maintenance, and where a bank is slipping, retaining walls that hold the ground in place.

### [Landscape Designer](/landscape-designer)

Patios and lawn seeding are often the first things people ask for when a garden needs remaking. Our [landscape designer](/landscape-designer) work settles where each one goes, so the seating gets the afternoon sun and the new lawn gets light where it's needed.

### [Tree Service](/tree-service)

Once a tree has come down and left a stump, most people want it cleared before they plant or lay anything in its place. Here's what a [tree service](/tree-service) does: tree stump removal, tree planting, and tree cabling and bracing for trees that need extra support.

### [Paving Contractor](/paving-contractor)

Rushed paving shows within a season, with uneven edges and joints that fill with weeds. Booking a [paving contractor](/paving-contractor) with us means block paving, concrete bases and paths laid to be used, not just to look right on the day.

### [Deck Builder](/deck-builder)

Most people want a deck that catches the evening sun and gets used on warm nights. Getting there depends on the surface and the frame. The [deck builder](/deck-builder) side of what we do covers composite decking, pergolas for shade, and decking restoration for an old deck that's still sound underneath.
