A lawn mowing visit is for homeowners who want the grass cut, the edges tidied and the clippings dealt with. It leaves the lawn at an even height with a clean line against the beds, and the paths clear.

A visit starts with a walk over the grass, lifting anything lying on it, because a stray stone can chip a blade or get thrown. Too much taken off in one cut does harm. Taking off more than a third of the height at once tears the blades instead of slicing them, and the tips turn pale and ragged. A long lawn is usually brought down in stages for that reason. The height setting matters as much as the mowing itself.

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Long grass with the beds starting to vanish into it is the sign a visit was missed while the lawn was growing. How quickly it shows depends on the weather and how hard the grass is pushing. Mowing brings the height back down, whereas long grass cut hard gets scalped, and its clumps shade what's underneath.

### It looks untidy from the house

From the window or the door, it's grass spilling over the path edge, a ragged line against the bed and clumps of cuttings left lying. Edges creep out between cuts, fastest in late spring when the lawn is growing hardest. The visit cuts the edge back to its line and deals with the clippings.

### It keeps coming back

Moss after a wet winter, or a flush of growth after warm rain, is normal. A weed that comes back after a treatment in the growing season isn't, because only what showed was dealt with. Mowing only takes off the top, so the cause is treated separately, in spring or autumn while the plant is growing.

### What the season is about to throw at it

The spring flush, when the soil warms and the grass grows all at once, is the season doing what it does, not a fault. Getting ahead of it means walking the lawn for stones and fallen twigs first. Then the cutting height is set to suit the fresh growth before the mower goes over it.

## How We Price Lawn Mowing Visits in Southampton

The amount of lawn to mow moves the price more than anything else, because it sets how long each visit takes. Once that's clear, a price is agreed ahead of the first cut.

### What a visit has to get through

Grass that's wet or has grown long takes more getting through. Wet grass clogs the mower and cuts unevenly. Damp cuttings are heavy to collect. The same lawn therefore takes longer after rain than in a dry spell.

### How much is in the way

Trees, play equipment and beds to work round slow the mower down, and so does a slope. A narrow gate or no side access means the mower and clippings are carried further, which adds time too.

### The first visit

A lawn that hasn't been cut for a whole season makes the first visit a bigger job. There's longer grass to bring down and more to clear. After that, each visit is priced as a visit, on the lawn as it then stands.

### Extras you add to the visit

Whatever goes on top of the mow is its own piece of work, so it adds to the visit. A bare patch resown with lawn seeding, for example, costs more as the patch gets bigger, because there's more ground to cover.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on how fast the grass grows, and the pattern is agreed with you. The mowing season runs from March to October, and mowing is usually paused in a prolonged summer drought or when the ground is very wet or frozen. Growth speeds up and slows down with the weather, which is why there's no single rule for every lawn.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

The size of the lawn moves the price most. A small back lawn and a long front lawn take very different times to cut and edge, so each one gets its own figure. To get yours, ask for a free quote and send a few photos of the lawn.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m for lawn mowing. The insurance is held for the mowing work we carry out in your garden. If you'd like to know more about the policy, just ask when you request a quote.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

Whether you need to be in depends on how the lawn is reached. A back garden with a side gate that opens from outside is a different case from one reached only through the house. Which applies to yours is settled with you when you book, so the arrangements for getting in are sorted before the first visit.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit covers the whole mow, from checking the lawn to leaving the paths clear. Loose debris comes off first, and the blade height is set before mowing. The lawn borders are trimmed back, and the clippings are bagged up or left to break down. Hedges aren't part of a mowing visit, but [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) can be added to one.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

No, there's nothing to apply for before a lawn gets cut. Grounds maintenance does have industry standards. In most cases the part that applies depends on the kind of area, such as lawns, hard surfaces or planted beds. They describe how the work is normally done, not one schedule that suits every garden.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

Mowing should begin once the grass has started growing again in spring. A lawn that's still dormant has little to cut, and growth picks up as the soil warms. Mild springs bring that point earlier and cold ones later, which is why it shifts from year to year.
