## What's Included in a Lawn Mowing Visit

Lawn mowing is the cutting of a home lawn, from checking the grass over to trimming the edges and dealing with the clippings. It suits homeowners who want the grass kept at a sensible height, with tidy edges where it meets beds and borders.

- **Inspect lawn and remove debris**: Lawn checked and debris cleared before cutting
- **Set cutting height**: Cutting height set to suit the lawn
- **Mow**: Rotary or cylinder mowing across the whole lawn
- **Edge or strim**: Lawn edges and tight corners cut back
- **Collect or disperse clippings**: Clippings collected or mulched back into the lawn
- **Clear paths**: Clearing stray grass from the paths

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Long grass and clumps of old cuttings are what a missed visit looks like when the lawn is growing fastest, and it shows quickly. A visit starts by checking the lawn and setting the cutting height. The longer it's left, the harder it has to be cut, which can scalp the grass, and the clumps shade what's beneath.

### It looks untidy from the house

From the house, it's the edges that give it away: grass lapping over the path and a ragged line where the lawn meets the bed. Between cuts the edge growth creeps outwards, fastest in late spring. A visit cuts the edge back to its line and collects the clippings or spreads them, as agreed.

### It keeps coming back

Fresh growth after warm rain is normal, so a lawn can look shaggy again soon. One that stays patchy or pale after every cut is different. The cause is the cutting, with the blades set too low or too much cut at once. Setting the height first and taking off no more than a third is how that's avoided.

### What the season is about to throw at it

Spring growth arrives all at once as the soil warms, and that's the season doing what it does, not a fault in the lawn. Getting ahead of it means cutting while the grass is still short. The mower height is set before the first pass, and the edges are trimmed once the lawn is done.

## How We Price Lawn Mowing Visits in Southampton

How much lawn there is to cut and edge moves the price more than anything else on a visit. Once we've heard what the lawn needs, you'll have a price in hand.

### What a visit has to get through

The area of grass and how high it has grown are what a visit has to get through. A wide lawn means more passes with the mower, and thick or damp growth clogs the blades, which slows the machine.

### How much is in the way

A lawn with trees, play equipment, beds or slopes on it takes longer than an open one. The mower has to keep turning and stopping to work round each. Narrow access, such as a tight side gate, adds time too.

### The first visit

A lawn that's been left for a season needs a bigger first visit. The long growth has to be cut down in stages before it can be mown at the right height. After that, it's an ordinary visit.

### Extras you add to the visit

Anything added on top of the cut is a separate piece of work, with its own time and materials. Sowing seed into thin patches or spreading bark on a bed are examples, and each raises the price of the visit.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on how fast the grass is growing. Growth follows the weather and the type of grass. A lawn in a warm, wet spell needs cutting more often than one in a dry spell. Mowing runs through the growing season and is paused in a long drought. The pattern for your lawn is agreed with you.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

It depends mostly on the lawn's size, as a bigger lawn means more cutting and edging. The quickest way to a figure is to ask for a quote, which is free. Sending photos of the lawn, if you have them, helps.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m. That cover is held for the mowing work itself. It takes in the cutting, the edging and the clearing up that make up a visit to your lawn.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

The arrangements for getting in are agreed before the first visit. What the work itself needs is a clear route to the lawn. That might be a side gate or a way through the house, and the arrangement is built around it.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit covers checking the lawn, setting the height, cutting and edging. Anything lying on the grass is picked up first, then the mower height is set and the lawn is cut. The edges are tidied back to their line, and the clippings are either taken away or left to break down. Hedges are separate work, though [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) can be added alongside.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

There's nothing to apply for, and no single rule sets how often to cut. Garden maintenance work usually follows a grounds maintenance standard, which covers areas such as lawns, hard surfaces and planting. It treats each part of a garden on its own terms. The way a lawn is cut is matched to that lawn, not to a fixed timetable.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

As soon as the grass has started growing again. The first cut of the year goes in once there's new growth to cut, after the grass has come out of its winter rest. A lawn that isn't growing yet doesn't need cutting, and an early cut gains little, as there's nothing to take off.
