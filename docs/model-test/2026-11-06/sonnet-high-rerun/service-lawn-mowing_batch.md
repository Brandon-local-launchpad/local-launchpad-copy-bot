## What's Included in a Lawn Mowing Visit

This is grass cutting for homeowners who want an even, neat lawn without doing it themselves. The job involves checking the grass and cutting it to a suitable height. It finishes with trimmed edges, which leaves a level cut right up to the borders.

- **Inspect lawn and remove debris**: Lawn checked and debris cleared away
- **Set cutting height**: Height of cut chosen for the grass
- **Mow**: Rotary or cylinder mowing across the whole lawn
- **Edge or strim**: Grass trimmed along paths, beds and fence lines
- **Collect or disperse clippings**: Clippings collected, or mulched back into the grass
- **Clear paths**: Path clearing once the mowing is finished

Cutting is where things go wrong when it's rushed. Grass grows from near the soil, so a mower set too low scalps it down to bare ground. A blade that tears instead of cutting is the other risk, because the torn tips brown back to where they were damaged. Either way the lawn is left stressed and patchy, with pale or brown marks that stay visible while it recovers.

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Long grass and heaps of cuttings left on the lawn are the usual signs. From spring to midsummer the lawn grows fastest, so a missed visit shows sooner than it would later in the year. Left long, it cuts unevenly, and a single hard pass can scalp it. A visit brings the lawn back down and clears what's left.

### It looks untidy from the house

From the window, it's the edge that shows first: grass spilling over the path, a ragged line against the bed, clumps left on the lawn. Between cuts the edge creeps outward, quickest in late spring when the lawn is growing hardest. A visit cuts it back to its line and deals with the clippings, collected or mulched in.

### It keeps coming back

A flush of fresh growth after warm rain is normal. What isn't normal is the same heaps of cuttings or ragged patches after every cut. That points to the cause being missed while only what shows is tidied. A visit checks the lawn first, then collects the clippings or spreads them thinly.

### What the season is about to throw at it

When the soil warms in spring, the grass wakes up and grows all at once. That's the season doing what it does, not a fault. Getting ahead of it looks like the lawn checked and the height set, then a clean pass made. The edges get cut back while the grass is still short, not once it's long.

Long or wet growth does most to push a price up, because it's slower to cut and heavier to clear. Once the job is clear, the figure is settled ahead of the first cut.

A larger lawn means more passes of the mower, and grass that's long or wet is slow going and leaves more cuttings to clear. Both add time to the visit, and that's what the price follows.

Trees, beds, play equipment and a narrow gate all slow the mower, because every one means turning, stopping and working round it. A steep bank takes slower, more careful passes, and each of these adds time to the visit.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on how fast the grass grows, and we agree the pattern with you. The season usually runs from March to October, with the weather and the type of grass moving it either way. Mowing eases off in a prolonged dry spell and is usually avoided on very wet or frozen ground.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

It depends mostly on how long the grass has grown, so prices vary by lawn. Two lawns of the same size can quote very differently for that reason. A quote is free, and a few photos of the lawn as it looks now give us a clearer picture to price from.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m for our gardening work, lawn mowing included. A mowing visit means powered mowers and strimmers working close to plants, walls and gates. That's the kind of work a policy like this is held for.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

That depends on how the lawn is reached. The arrangements for getting in are agreed before the first visit. Mowing itself is done out on the grass, so what counts is a clear way through to it. That might be a side gate, a key or a door left open.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit covers cutting the lawn and leaving it tidy. Anything lying on the grass is picked up first, then the blades are set and the cut is made. The edges are trimmed back, and the clippings and paths are dealt with. Hedges and weeds aren't part of a mowing visit, and [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) is booked separately.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

There's nothing you need to apply for to have your lawn mown. Grounds maintenance work is usually done to a recognised standard that covers things like grass, hard areas and planted beds. It doesn't set one cutting schedule for every lawn, so there's no rule on how often a lawn must be cut.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first visit of the year goes in once the grass has started growing again. Before that there's little to cut. A mild spell or a cold snap can bring growth on early or hold it back, so it isn't tied to a calendar date. Lawns in sheltered spots usually start sooner than exposed ones.
