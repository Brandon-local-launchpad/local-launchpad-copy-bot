## Your Landscaping Questions Answered

### How much does landscaping cost in Southampton?

The size of the area is what moves the price most. A small patio and a whole back garden need very different amounts of materials and time, so no single figure covers both. The quickest way to a real price for your garden is to ask us for a quote, with a few photos of the space.

### Are you insured?

Yes, we're insured for up to £5m. The cover is for accidental damage to your property and injury to other people. That applies while the work is going on, such as a branch falling during tree work. Whether a particular incident falls within the policy is a decision for the insurer, under the policy's terms and conditions.

### What happens if something isn't right after the job?

Tell us what isn't right and we'll look at it with you. Reasonable care and skill is the standard every trade works to, and it's the standard our work is held to. If a job falls short of it, we want to hear so we can look at the problem properly.

### How soon can you start?

We can usually start the same week you book the job. To check which days we have free, call us on 07580 412717 and we'll go through them with you.

### Is the quote free and no obligation?

Yes, quotes are free with no obligation to go ahead. Asking for a quote doesn't commit you to anything, so you can take it away and think it over in your own time.

### Which areas do you cover?

We cover Southampton and up to 20 miles from it, inland and along the coast. That takes in Chandlers Ford, Hedge End, Lyndhurst and Kings Worthy, whether the job is our [gardener](/gardener) work or a bigger project. If you're unsure whether you're in range, just ask us.

### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

A reasonable quote makes clear what work is included and what materials are used. For a patio, that means the area being laid, the paving chosen and whether taking away the old surface and waste is covered. If any of that is vague, ask for it to be explained.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

It's usually slow ground, a surface that slopes the wrong way, or no drain. Some Southampton gardens sit on clay, and ground nearer the Test and Itchen can stay wet. Two gardens on the same road can drain differently. The fix depends on which cause applies: correcting the fall of the surface, or adding a drain or soakaway to carry the water off.
