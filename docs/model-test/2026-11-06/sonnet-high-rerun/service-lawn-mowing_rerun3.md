## What's Included in a Lawn Mowing Visit

A lawn mowing visit cuts the grass at a set height, trims the edges and clears up the clippings. It's for homeowners who want the lawn kept at a steady, even height without doing the cutting themselves.

- **Inspect lawn and remove debris**: Lawn checked and debris cleared before cutting
- **Set cutting height**: Blade height adjusted for the grass
- **Mow**: Rotary or cylinder mowing across the lawn
- **Edge or strim**: Lawn edges cut back along paths and beds
- **Collect or disperse clippings**: Clippings collected or mulched back into the lawn
- **Clear paths**: Paths swept clear of cuttings

The lawn is cut with a rotary mower, which spins a blade flat over the grass, or a cylinder mower, which shears it against a fixed bar. It's the cut itself that goes wrong when it's rushed. A lawn that loses more than about a third of its height in one go has its tips torn rather than sliced. Those torn tips turn pale and brown for a while before the grass recovers. That's why the cutting height is matched to the grass in front of it rather than left on one setting.

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Grass long enough to flop over usually means a visit has been missed. In the growing season, when grass is at its fastest, a missed cut soon shows. Cut hard in one go, long grass gets scalped. The clumps of cuttings then shade the lawn beneath, which is why long grass is brought down gradually.

### It looks untidy from the house

From the door you notice grass over the edge of the path. You'll also see a ragged line against the bed and clumps of cuttings on the lawn. Between cuts the edges creep outward, fastest in late spring when the lawn grows hardest. A visit cuts the edge back to its line and collects or spreads the clippings.

### It keeps coming back

A flush of growth after warm rain is normal, not a fault. What isn't normal is pale, worn patches that reappear after every cut. That usually points to a cutting height set too low, and trimming again only deals with what shows. A visit deals with the cause by setting the blade height for the lawn while it's growing.

### What the season is about to throw at it

When the soil warms in spring, grass puts on growth almost all at once, and that's the season doing what it does, not a fault. Getting ahead of it means cutting at a height set for the new growth. The clippings are then collected or mulched back in, so the surge doesn't pile up.

## How We Price Lawn Mowing Visits in Southampton

How much grass there is to cut moves the price more than anything else. The price is settled with you ahead of the first visit, so you have a clear figure to say yes or no to.

### What a visit has to get through

A bigger lawn takes longer to cut, and an awkwardly shaped one takes longer again. Long or wet growth is slower to get through and leaves more to collect, so it costs more than a lawn that's been kept short.

### How much is in the way

Trees, play equipment, beds and furniture all mean the mower has to stop and turn, and each one gets trimmed round by hand. A steep slope or a narrow gate slows the machine too, and all of it adds time.

### The first visit

Grass left for a whole season needs a bigger first visit, because there's more growth to bring back under control. After that, each visit is priced as a visit, and growth that's kept manageable costs less to cut.

### Extras you add to the visit

What an extra costs depends on how big it is. A short stretch of hedge trimming adds little to a visit, a long boundary hedge adds much more, and a bed to mulch grows with its area.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on how fast the grass grows, so we agree the pattern with you. Growth follows the weather and the type of grass, and the cutting season runs from March to October. In a long dry spell mowing is usually paused. The same goes for very wet or frozen ground, because cutting stressed or sodden grass does more harm than good.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

It depends most on how much lawn there is to cut. That's why there's no single price that fits every garden. The quickest way to a figure is to ask for a free quote and send a few photos of the lawn. You'll have a figure in hand before the first visit.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m. That cover goes with the mowing, edging and clearing we carry out in your garden.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

That's settled with you before the first visit, along with how we get in. Side gates, locks and whether the lawn is at the front or the back all affect it. Agreeing it beforehand means the day itself doesn't stall at a locked gate.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A lawn mowing visit covers these stages: inspect lawn and remove debris, set cutting height, mow, edge or strim, collect or disperse clippings and clear paths. We also do [fencing](/services/fencing), [lawn seeding](/services/lawn-seeding) and [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming). You're left with a lawn at an even height and paths you can walk along.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

No, there's nothing you need to apply for or sign off. Grounds maintenance standards usually treat lawns, hard surfaces and planted areas as separate parts. They describe how the work is done rather than fixing one routine for every garden. In most cases the cutting simply follows what the grass needs on the day.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first visit of the year goes in once the grass has started growing again. Until then it's dormant, and cutting only takes off leaf that can't regrow yet, which leaves the lawn thin and tired. Once new growth shows, a first cut tidies the lawn and sets it up for the months ahead. You can add [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) to that visit too.
