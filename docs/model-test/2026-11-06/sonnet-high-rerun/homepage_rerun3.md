## Our Landscaping Services in Southampton

This is landscaping for homeowners in Southampton and the towns around it, covering upkeep, trees, paving and decking. Large family gardens with mature trees sit alongside compact town gardens, and the work below suits both.

### [Gardener](/gardener)

Hedges that have outgrown their line, a fence that needs replacing and weeds in the beds all wear a garden down. Our [gardener](/gardener) work covers fencing, hedge trimming and weed control, so the boundaries and borders get looked after together.

### [Landscape Gardener](/landscape-gardener)

Who can look after the lawn and the garden walls as well as the borders? That's what a [landscape gardener](/landscape-gardener) visit covers: garden maintenance, lawn care and retaining walls, which hold back the soil where the garden changes level.

### [Landscape Designer](/landscape-designer)

Patios and lawn seeding are the two jobs that turn bare ground into a garden you can sit in. You can ask for [landscape designer](/landscape-designer) work from us when you'd like to talk through the plan as well as the building.

### [Tree Service](/tree-service)

Once the leaves have dropped in autumn, a tree's shape is easy to read, which makes it a sensible time to arrange work on it. In practice, what a [tree service](/tree-service) does is tree stump removal, tree planting and tree cabling and bracing, which supports a heavy limb.

### [Paving Contractor](/paving-contractor)

Paving that's badly laid tilts, cracks or sinks, and then someone has to dig it up. Booking a [paving contractor](/paving-contractor) with us covers block paving, concrete bases and paths, the hard surfaces that carry cars, bins and everyday foot traffic.

### [Deck Builder](/deck-builder)

Most people want a deck or seating area they'll actually use. The [deck builder](/deck-builder) side of what we do gets there through pergolas, composite decking and decking restoration. Pergolas frame the seating, composite decking lays a new surface, and restoration brings an old deck back into use.

## Your Landscaping Questions Answered

### How much does landscaping cost in Southampton?

The size of the job moves the price most. A border tidy and a full patio are very different amounts of work, so costs vary a lot. Ask us for a quote, with photos of the garden if you have them. You'll get a figure for your own job rather than a guess.

### Are you insured?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m. The cover is there for accidental damage to your home or garden and injury to other people while work is going on. What the policy covers in any one case depends on its terms and the circumstances.

### What happens if something isn't right after the job?

Tell us what isn't right and we'll look at it. All trade work is held to a standard of reasonable care and skill, and that's the standard ours is held to. If something falls short of it, say so and we'll go through what went wrong.

### How soon can you start?

We can usually start the same week. If you've a particular week in mind, mention it when you contact us and we'll let you know whether that works for us.

### Is the quote free and no obligation?

Yes, quotes are free and you're under no obligation to go ahead. Saying no afterwards costs you nothing either. Once you've had the figure, the decision is entirely yours.

### Which areas do you cover?

We cover Southampton and everywhere within 20 miles, inland and along the coast. That includes Eastleigh, Winchester, Romsey and Ampfield. If your town isn't named here, ring us on 07580 412717 and we'll tell you whether we can reach you.

### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

A reasonable quote spells out exactly what work it covers. If it just says "patio", you can't tell whether digging out, taking away the old surface or edging are in the price. Where anything is vague, ask what it means before agreeing, because that's where extra charges tend to appear.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually the ground underneath drains slowly, but there can be more than one cause. The others are a surface that falls the wrong way, or no drain or soakaway to carry the water off. Some Southampton gardens sit on clay that holds water, and gardens close to the rivers and the coast can stay wet for longer. Which cause it is decides the fix, and that's a job for a [landscape gardener](/landscape-gardener).
