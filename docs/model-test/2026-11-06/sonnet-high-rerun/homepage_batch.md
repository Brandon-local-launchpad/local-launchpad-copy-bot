## Your Landscaping Questions Answered

### How much does landscaping cost in Southampton?

It depends most on how much digging and building the job involves. Tidying a border costs far less than the work a [landscape gardener](/landscape-gardener) takes on, such as a retaining wall that holds soil back. Since gardens differ, a quote for yours is the way to get a real figure.

### Are you insured?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m. The cover is for accidental damage to your property and injury to other people while the work is going on. What a claim involves depends on the policy terms and who is at fault. Tell us straight away if anything is damaged.

### What happens if something isn't right after the job?

Tell us what isn't right and we'll look at it. Every trader is expected to work with reasonable care and skill, and that's the standard our work is held to. If something falls short of it, the sooner you tell us, the easier it is to look into.

### How soon can you start?

We can usually start the same week. The day itself is agreed with you, so if you have a date in mind, mention it when you call or ask for a quote.

### Is the quote free and no obligation?

Yes, quotes are free and there's no obligation. Work only starts once you've agreed the price with us, so asking for a quote commits you to nothing.

### Which areas do you cover?

We cover Southampton and everywhere within 20 miles of it, inland and along the coast. That includes Hedge End, Chilworth, Stockbridge and Kings Worthy. If your town isn't named, ask us, because the distance is what counts.

### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

It's reasonable when it clearly shows what you're paying for. A quote should say what work is being done and which materials are used, with nothing left as a vague lump sum. For example, a fence quote should give the length, the height and whether old panels are being taken away.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually the water has nowhere to go, for one of three reasons. The ground underneath may drain slowly, the surface may fall the wrong way, or there may be no drain or soakaway. Southampton's ground isn't the same everywhere, and low lying spots near the rivers can stay wet for longer through winter. Each cause has a different fix, from changing the fall of the surface to adding drainage.
