## What's Included in a Lawn Mowing Visit

Lawn mowing is the cutting of a lawn at a set height, with the edges trimmed and the clippings dealt with, for homeowners who want grass kept even. Each visit leaves the lawn cut level, the edges neat and the paths clear of cuttings.

- **Inspect lawn and remove debris**: Lawn checked and loose debris cleared before cutting
- **Set cutting height**: Cutting height set for the current grass length
- **Mow**: Grass mown across the whole lawn
- **Edge or strim**: Edges cut back where the mower can't reach
- **Collect or disperse clippings**: Clippings collected or mulched back into the lawn
- **Clear paths**: Stray clippings removed from paths and hard surfaces

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Long grass and thick clumps of cuttings are what a missed visit leaves in the growing season. That runs from spring to midsummer, when the lawn grows fastest. Cut hard in one go, tall grass gets scalped to bare stems, and the clumps block light from what's underneath. A visit sets the height to suit the growth before mowing.

### It looks untidy from the house

What shows from the window is the edge: grass spilling over the path, a ragged line against a bed, cuttings left lying on the lawn. Between cuts, edge growth creeps over paths and beds fastest in late spring, when the lawn is growing hardest. A visit cuts the edge back to its line and collects or spreads the clippings.

### It keeps coming back

A flush of growth after warm rain is normal. What isn't normal is a ragged look after every cut. It means the height was set too low or too much came off at once, so only what shows was dealt with. Taking a little off the height each cut, from March to October, tackles the cause.

### What the season is about to throw at it

As the soil warms in spring, grass that barely moved over winter starts growing all at once. That's the season doing what it does, not a fault. Getting ahead of it means mowing while the lawn is still short enough to cut cleanly. The height is set for the growth, and the edges are trimmed back.

## How We Price Lawn Mowing Visits in Southampton

How much grass there is moves the price of a visit more than anything else, because more lawn means more to cut, edge and clear. When you ask for a quote, the figure you're given is for your own lawn, not a general price.

### What a visit has to get through

A larger lawn means more ground to cover. Long or wet growth makes each pass heavier going, so a big lawn that's been left costs more than a small one that's kept short.

### How much is in the way

Trampolines, play equipment, beds cut into the lawn and narrow gates all hold up a visit. The mower has to be steered round each one. Steep or uneven ground slows the pace too, and a longer visit means a higher price.

### The first visit

A lawn left for a season needs a bigger first visit, because long growth takes longer to cut and leaves more cuttings to clear. After that, each visit is priced on its own, for the lawn as it then stands.

### Extras you add to the visit

Hedge trimming, weed control or mulching can be added to a visit, and lawn seeding where the grass has gone thin. Each is extra work on top of the cut, so each one adds to the price.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on how fast the grass grows, not on the calendar. A usual rule is to mow before the grass has grown about a third taller than the height it's kept at. Warm, damp weather gets it there sooner, and a dry spell slows it right down. The pattern for your lawn is agreed with you.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

The amount of grass to cut moves the price most. Ask for a free quote and send a few photos of the lawn, and the price you get will fit your garden.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m for the work we do. The cover relates to the lawn work itself: the cutting, the edging and the clearing involved in each visit. If you'd like to know more about it, ask when you request a quote.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

Whether you need to be home is agreed with you before the first visit. The same goes for how we get into the garden, so the way in is settled before the first cut.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit covers cutting the grass, trimming the edges and dealing with the clippings. It begins with a check of the lawn for debris, then the height is set and the grass mown. A strimmer finishes the corners and lawn edges. Hedges aren't part of a mowing visit, but [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming) can be booked alongside it.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

No, there's nothing to apply for to have a lawn cut. Grounds maintenance has an industry standard. In most cases it treats lawns, hard surfaces, planted beds and water areas as separate parts of the work. It doesn't fix one rule on how often every lawn is cut. The grass itself decides that.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first cut is due once the grass has started growing again. Until then there's little length to take off, so an early cut does nothing for the lawn. A lawn that's started growing shows it in the length of the grass, and that's the point for the first visit.
