## Your Landscaping Questions Answered

### How much does landscaping cost in Southampton?

It depends mostly on the size of the job and how much digging it involves. A tidy up of one border and a new patio sit a long way apart, because digging means removing soil and bringing in materials. The quickest way to a real figure is to ask for a quote and send us a few photos of the garden.

### Are you insured?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m. Landscaping means digging, lifting slabs and working close to walls, drains and trees. That's where accidental damage or an injury to someone nearby can happen. Cover of this kind exists for those events. What the insurer decides in a particular case depends on the terms of the policy and what happened.

### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

A reasonable quote spells out what's included, so you can see where the money goes. One line reading "patio, one sum" tells you little, while a quote that separates labour from materials lets you see what each part costs.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually slow ground, a surface sloping the wrong way, or no drain for the water. Southampton's ground varies, and some of it, especially near the rivers and the coast, drains slowly or sits wet when groundwater is high. A [landscape gardener](/landscape-gardener) can work out which cause it is, and whether a new fall, a drain or a soakaway is the fix.
