## What's Included in a Lawn Mowing Visit

A lawn mowing visit takes the grass down to a chosen height, then trims the edges and clears the paths around it. It's for homeowners with a lawn that needs regular cutting, and it leaves the garden looking kept, with the clippings dealt with.

- **Inspect lawn and remove debris**: Walk the lawn and clear sticks and other debris
- **Set cutting height**: Adjust the mower to the right cutting height
- **Mow**: Cut the lawn with a rotary or cylinder mower
- **Edge or strim**: Cut back grass along edges and in tight corners
- **Collect or disperse clippings**: Collect the clippings or mulch them back into the lawn
- **Clear paths**: Clear loose grass off the paths

The lawn is then cut with a rotary mower, which has one flat spinning blade, or a cylinder mower, which cuts like scissors using a drum of blades. What matters most is how much comes off in one pass. Take away more than about a third of the height and the grass is torn and stressed instead of cut cleanly. A blade set too low shaves down to the crown, the base of the plant where new growth starts, which leaves pale, thin patches that are slow to fill back in. That's why the height is settled before the first pass, not after it.

## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

Grass that's got long usually means a visit was missed while the lawn was growing. Between spring and midsummer, even a missed fortnight shows. Cut hard after that, long grass gets scalped, and clumps of clippings shade the lawn underneath. A visit sets the cutting height first, then mows and clears up.

### It looks untidy from the house

From the door or kitchen window, the edges are what you notice first. Grass laps over the path, the line against the bed goes ragged, and clumps lie on the lawn. The edge grows over between cuts, most in late spring. A visit trims it back to its line and clears the clippings, though the grass keeps growing.

### It keeps coming back

Grass shooting back soon after a cut is normal, especially after warm rain. A patch that's thin or yellow every time isn't. That points to a cause nobody dealt with, such as clippings left lying in heaps. In the growing season, a visit sets the height right and clears the clumps.

### What the season is about to throw at it

The first big flush of grass comes all at once in spring, as the soil warms. That's the season doing what it does, not a fault in the lawn. Getting ahead of it means mowing while the grass is still short. Then the mower takes only the tips off, at a cutting height chosen for the lawn.

## How We Price Lawn Mowing Visits in Southampton

How much growth there is to get through, along with the area of grass to cover, moves the price more than anything else. Cutting and clearing more grass takes more time on the day, so the price follows the amount of work in the lawn.

### What a visit has to get through

A larger lawn takes longer to cover, and grass that's long or wet is slower to cut and heavier to clear. All of it means more work, so a big overgrown lawn costs more than a small, tidy one.

### How much is in the way

Beds to work round, slopes, a tree in the middle of the lawn and a narrow way in for the mower all add time. Every obstacle means more turning and more hand work around it, so the price goes up.

### The first visit

A lawn that's gone a whole season without a cut needs a bigger first visit, because there's far more grass to get through and clear. Once it's back under control, any visit after that is a smaller job and priced that way.

### Extras you add to the visit

Anything added to the cut is a separate piece of work, priced by its own size. A long hedge line takes more time than a short one, and a job that uses materials, such as a lawn feed, brings their cost in too.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

There's no fixed number, because the grass sets the pace. Mowing follows how fast it's growing, so cuts come closer together in warm, damp weather and spread out in a dry spell. Cutting pauses in prolonged drought and on very wet or frozen ground, when a mower does more harm than good. The pattern for your lawn is agreed with you.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

The amount of grass to be cut moves the price most. A small front lawn is a different job from a long back garden, and the figure reflects the difference. To get a figure for yours, ask for a quote, which is free.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A lawn mowing visit covers these stages: inspect lawn and remove debris, set cutting height, mow, edge or strim, collect or disperse clippings and clear paths. We also do [fencing](/services/fencing), [lawn seeding](/services/lawn-seeding) and [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming). The lawn is left at one cutting height, with a clean line along the paths.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

Yes, there's a grounds maintenance standard, but it describes how the work is usually done. It has separate parts for hard areas, grass, planting and water, so no single pattern of cutting suits every garden. A lawn follows the part that covers grass, and there's nothing for you to apply for.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first visit of the year goes in once the grass has started growing again. Grass only grows when the soil has warmed, so before then there's little length to take off and a cut achieves very little. The first cut is usually a light one, and bare patches left by winter are a job for [lawn seeding](/services/lawn-seeding).
