## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

From spring to midsummer, once the soil has warmed, grass grows faster than at any other time, so a missed visit shows quickly. Its pace depends on the weather, the lawn's size and time since the last cut. Left too long, it must come down in one hard cut that strips too much leaf. Regular visits keep cuts small.

### It looks untidy from the house

Look out of the kitchen window and the signs are usually at the edges. Grass reaches over the path, and the line against the border loses its shape. Edges creep back between cuts, and late spring is when they move fastest. Edging puts the line back, but the grass at the border starts reaching out again.

### It keeps coming back

Some regrowth is expected. Moss after a wet winter, or fresh growth after warm rain, needs no treatment. A weed that returns quickly after treatment is different. Treating only what shows leaves the cause in place. Treatment is timed for when the plant is growing, in spring or autumn.

### What the season is about to throw at it

Spring growth often arrives all at once, as grass, hedges and beds wake together. It's the season at work, not a fault in the lawn. Booked before the flush peaks, the first cut sets the blades high and takes only a light off the top. After that, the visits hold it there.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

It depends on how fast the grass is growing, which changes with the weather. The cut usually runs from March to October, and it pauses in a prolonged summer drought or on very wet or frozen ground. How often that works out for your lawn is agreed with you, based on how fast it's growing.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

Growth since the last cut moves the price most. Grass left to build up takes more passes, while a lawn cut often stays quicker each time. Ask for a free quote for your lawn and we'll give you a figure for it.

### Are you insured for lawn mowing?

Yes, we're insured up to £5m. The cover is for the work we do on your property, so it applies to a lawn mowing visit as much as any other job. If you'd like the details before booking, just let us know and we'll explain them.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

RETURN: faq_answer: That's agreed before the first visit. How the mower gets into your garden is settled ahead of the cut. That covers whether you need to be in, and any side gate or key the job needs.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit is the cut itself, with the edges and the clippings included. Walking the grass first, setting the height and trimming the lawn's margins all sit inside one visit. Hedge trimming and weed control aren't part of the cut, but you can add either to the same visit, such as [hedge trimming](/services/hedge-trimming).

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

Yes, the cut usually follows a recognised approach for each grass area. It covers fine lawns and rougher grass alike, setting the cutting height and the way each kind is cut. Nothing needs applying for on your side.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first cut goes in once the grass starts growing again. Exact timing moves with the weather, so there's no set date. Before then there's little growth to cut, and cutting frozen or sodden ground tears the grass rather than helping it.
