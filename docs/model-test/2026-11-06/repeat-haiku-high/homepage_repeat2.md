### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

A reasonable quote says what's included, and what isn't, in plain words. Look for a clear description of the work: its size, what's being built or removed, and the materials. A single figure with no description tells you nothing about the work, so there's nothing to judge the price against.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually because the ground drains slowly or the surface falls the wrong way. Water can also sit where there's no drain or soakaway to take it away. Parts of Southampton have slow draining soil, often low land near the water, so the ground itself may be the problem. Checking the wettest spot shows which of these it is, and so which fix will work.
