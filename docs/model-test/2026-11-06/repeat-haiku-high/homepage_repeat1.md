It depends on the job, and digging out ground moves the price most. Old paving or soil has to be lifted and hauled away, which adds to the bill. ### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

It depends on what the quote includes, not just the total. A quote that lists each part, such as the edging, the paving and the planting, shows what you're paying for. Lump sums hide that, so it's harder to tell what's been left out. If a fence, patio or deck might need permission, check with the council, as it depends on the house and where it sits.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Usually the ground underneath is slow to let water through. Sometimes the surface falls the wrong way and gathers water in one spot. Or there's no drain or soakaway to carry it off. Near the rivers, the soil can stay wet longer after heavy rain. Which one applies decides the fix, from levelling a low spot to giving the water a way out.
