## Signs It's Time for a Lawn Mowing Visit

### Growth has got ahead of you

A lawn missed through the growing season soon shows it: grass past the edging, a hedge over the path, beds overrun. Speed depends on how warm it's been, how much rain has fallen and how long since the last cut. Once it's got that far, the next visit is a bigger job than a routine cut.

### It looks untidy from the house

From the window, grass spilling over the path and a ragged edge against the bed show first. Between cuts, that growth creeps over paths and beds, fastest in late spring. A visit trims the growth back to the line and deals with the clippings. It won't stay tidy for long, as the grass soon pushes out again.

### It keeps coming back

Some growth coming back is normal. Moss after a wet winter, or a flush of green after warm rain, needs no treatment. A weed that returns soon after treatment means the cause underneath is still there. Treatment is timed to the plant's growing season, in spring or autumn.

### What the season is about to throw at it

A lawn that jumps in April is the season doing what it does, not a fault. As the soil warms, grass puts on its heaviest growth of the year. Getting ahead of it means a visit as the growth starts, cutting back what's there while it's still easy to cut.

## Lawn Mowing Questions Answered

### How often should lawn mowing be done?

Visits follow how fast the grass grows, not a set number of weeks. Lawns are usually cut from March to October, and the schedule is agreed with you. In a drought, or on very wet or frozen ground, mowing is cut back or paused until conditions ease.

### How much does a lawn mowing visit cost in Southampton?

Lawn size and how overgrown it is move the price most. A lawn left to get long costs more to bring back than one kept up. Ask us for a free quote, and we'll work out the figure for your garden from there.

### Do I need to be home for a lawn mowing visit?

How we get in is arranged with you before the first visit. Anything we need to know to reach the lawn is worth telling us then, so the arrangements are set before we start.

### What does a lawn mowing visit include?

A visit covers the cut, the edges, the clippings and the paths. The grass is cut to a set height. Edges are trimmed back from the paths, and the cuttings are dealt with as part of the visit. Fencing isn't part of a mowing visit, so it's booked as its own job on our [fencing](/services/fencing) page.

### Are there rules or standards for lawn mowing?

You don't need to apply for anything to have a lawn mown. Grounds work is usually done to published industry practice. That practice covers many kinds of ground, from ornamental grass to paths and hard surfaces, which is why there's no single timetable for every garden. The cut is set to how the grass is growing, and that's the part you'd see us follow.

### When should lawn mowing start for the year?

The first cut of the year is set by the growth, not a calendar. It comes once the grass has started to grow and the ground is workable, so mild weather and unfrozen soil decide it. A cold spell can hold it back, and it moves from one season to the next.
