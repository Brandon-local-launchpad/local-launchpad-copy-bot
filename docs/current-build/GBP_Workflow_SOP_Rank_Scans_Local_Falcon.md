## The headline metric: Top 3%

Current: Top 3% is the share of LeadSnap grid points where the client ranks 1st, 2nd or 3rd. We track it monthly against the baseline from step 1 to see whether the work is moving rankings.

New: Top 3% is the share of the rank map's kept pins where the client ranks 1st, 2nd or 3rd. Copybot works it out from Local Falcon's scan, over the pins inside the service area only. We track it monthly against the baseline from step 1 to see whether the work is moving rankings.

## Glossary, Top 3%

Current: The percentage of LeadSnap grid points where the client ranks 1st–3rd. Example: 51 of 169 points = 30%

New: The percentage of the rank map's kept pins where the client ranks 1st to 3rd. Example: 51 of 169 pins = 30%. A client not in the top 20 at any pin shows "not in the top 20" instead of an average rank.

## 1.7, replace the whole section

Title: 1.7 Run the baseline rank scan (Copybot, Local Falcon)

1. **Place ID first.** On the client's Record tab, open Google listing, find the listing and confirm it (with its address type: service area if the address is hidden). The scan matches the client by place ID only and won't run without it.
2. **Settings are set by Copybot, not by hand:** keyword = the primary category in lower case with no town (e.g. landscaper), plus an optional second keyword (scan_keyword_2 on Client Values); a 13 × 13 grid; spacing from scan_area on Client Values (dense city 1,000 m, city 1,500 m, market town 2,000 m, rural 2,500 m; the radius is the spacing × 6).
3. **Centre:** the GBP pin, or the main town for a service area business with a hidden address (that is the correct centre).
4. **Clipping:** Copybot keeps only the pins inside the service area towns plus 3 km. Local Falcon still charges for the whole grid (169 credits a keyword).
5. On the Rankings tab click **Run baseline scan**. The confirm shows where the centre came from, the points paid for and kept, the credits and the balance, and any towns left out. Read it, then run it.
6. The settings lock after this run. **Every monthly scan uses exactly these settings, and Copybot runs it by itself** on the same day each month.
7. The Top 3%, the average rank (or "not in the top 20"), the leader and the map are on the Rankings tab. **Export PNG** saves the map; **Open report** opens Local Falcon's shareable report.

Done when line, new: ☐ Place ID confirmed; baseline scan run on the Rankings tab
