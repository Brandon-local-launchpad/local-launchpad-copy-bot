  4. Origin story and market
  The offer was built from one to two months of cold calling local businesses to find their
  pain points.


  The research
  Brandon spent the best part of a month, two months, cold calling plumbers, electricians,
  drainage companies, HVAC companies and more. Two patterns emerged:

   1. They all hate Checkatrade. It has turned from a fair platform into a pay-to-win platform.
   2. There is a massive gap between those at the top of Google and those who are not.

  Example: an electrician in Bournemouth ranked number 2 on Google gets about 100 calls a
  week. On page 5 or 6, someone gets 5 calls a month, mostly scrapers and price shoppers,
  so he never gets the jobs he wants and has to take on jobs he does not want. That is what
  the offer was built around.


  Early results (launch, June 2026)
  The product was not there. The aim was to test demand, and there was demand.

  If a prospect watched the VSL in its entirety before the call, they were much more likely
  to buy. It answers the questions and pre-qualifies them.


  Target market
       Boomers. They do not understand the tech side, so calls moved from Google Meet to
       WhatsApp phone calls.
       TAM: roughly 500,000 to 750,000 businesses in the served niches across the UK
       (Brandon's estimate). Not going for all of them, but it is huge.
       Brandon does not expect ads to be limited even at £500,000 a day in spend. The
       constraint is delivery, not demand.
       Expansion to the US and Australia is possible once the UK is right.


  The constraint
  We are geared up on the getting-clients side. We are not geared up on delivery, because
  the product is not built yet. Many WordPress, website and local SEO agencies cap
  themselves at their supply constraint. Local Launchpad must not. To sign, say, 100 clients a
  month, a lot of delivery has to be automated.

  Why GoHighLevel was dropped
       A previous team did everything in GHL. The issue: there is not a ton that can be

       services needing 42 service pages meant 42 manual copy-and-paste jobs. Even at half

  What the copy bot produces
  A copywriting bot hosted on Railway outputs every piece of copy per page:

       H1s
       H2s
       H3s
       Meta descriptions
       Meta titles
       Schema
       The entire website copy
       What the internal linking should be
       The whole lot

  It takes about 20 minutes to feed the bot, including the client's onboarding form and all
  the information. The sitemap is fed into the Railway copy bot. It determines how many
  pages are needed from the sitemap and creates all the copy.

   1. Templates are built in WordPress (say a service page template).
   2. A client needs 42 service pages. Railway and WordPress talk to each other over the
      REST API and create those 42 pages from the template.
   3. The bot pushes the copy to each section of each of those 42 pages, and all the other
       pages needed.

  Editorial links versus navigation links
       Navigation and footer links barely pass authority. Google knows they are site structure,
       not endorsements.
       Editorial links, links inside the content, pass real authority. Google reads them as this
       page recommending that page.
       In Local Launchpad templates, editorial links are inline hyperlinked text inside body
       copy, never standalone buttons or card-level links. Every template needs at least one
       paragraph-level body copy field to carry them.

  Mandatory page rules (Local Launchpad house rules)
       FAQ sections never use collapsed accordions. Googlebot cannot expand them, so the

       SEO structure, not homepage layout alone. Template fingerprinting across clients in

  Writing the 30 pages
       Google does not care whether content is AI or human. It cares whether it is helpful.
       Most AI content is generic, vague, walls of text.
       Build with AI, then a human editor adds real business personality and voice, images
       (client photos best, AI-generated works), callouts, tables, headers, bullet points, short
       paragraphs and internal links.
       Google evaluates pages on the latest mobile Chrome. A wall of AI slop with no images
       and no formatting reads as a wall of AI slop.


  GBP handling (Local Launchpad rules)
       Duplicate services in GBP are selected under every applicable category. Each instance
       is an independent relevance signal.
       Categories to avoid or handle carefully: Landscape Architect (protected title in the
       UK), nationally dominated niches (e.g. solar with MCS accreditation), overly broad
       mixed-intent categories (e.g. "Builders").

  After launch: the decision loop
   1. 30 days after the Core 30 launches, run a rank map for the target keyword (primary
      category or a core service plus city).
   2. Calculate the top 3 percentage: the share of the map in positions 1 to 3. Position 4 and
       worse is invisible. LeadSnap gives this automatically.
   3. Below threshold: build supporting topical content. FAQs come from People Also Ask
       and from Reddit (a prompt crawls Reddit for real local questions). Add the question to
       the relevant Core 30 page, answer it in a couple dozen words, add an editorial link to a
       new in-depth supporting page. Keep going until the threshold is hit.
   4. At threshold: switch to geographical content. Build location pages, expanding outward
       from the address. Watch the green zone grow on the rank map.
   5. Dominating everywhere: maintain and defend. Keep GBP active, respond to every
       review, add services as the business evolves, consider another GBP location, monitor
       competitors.
   6. Repeat monthly. A second location gets a new GBP and a new Core 30.

  What it does

       location-category (the last uses the Category prompt).

   4. Assembles the prompt: CORE prompt for the page type, the trade's calibration pack, a

  approach; address what the owner already tried (YouTube methods, a gadget lead,
  treats); sell the capability not the dog; never guarantee behavioural outcomes or
  timelines. Section structure differs: problem training services get "Why It Isn't Going
  Away On Its Own" instead of Signs; life-stage services (puppy, adolescent, rescue) get "Is
  This Right For You"; security services get "When a Dog Unit Is the Right Call"; seasonal
  tasks become "Training Through the Life Stages" (puppy, adolescent, adult, senior) on
  training pages and nothing on security pages. The bot injects this as a trade override
  block.

  Edens_Edge_Custom_Values - HOW TO USE.csv is the HOW TO USE tab of a GHL-era
