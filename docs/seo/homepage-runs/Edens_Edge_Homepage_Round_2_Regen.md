### How do I know if a landscaping quote is reasonable?

It depends mainly on what the ground preparation involves. A patio that needs excavation and a proper base costs more than one laid on an existing surface. That's the base work that makes it last. Ask what the price includes below the surface: drainage and preparation are where corners get cut, and the effects show up months later.

### Why does water sit on my patio or lawn after rain?

Three separate things can cause it, and they often overlap: the ground beneath may absorb rainfall slowly, the surface may fall toward a low point rather than away from it, or there's no outlet to carry water away. Southampton's geology varies, and some areas sit on ground that holds water, particularly in lower spots near the rivers. A surface that lets water through only helps if what's underneath can receive it.
