### Page: Service Terms

Last updated: 7 November 2026

These terms apply to all plumbing and heating work carried out by Jane Plumbing. Please read them before you accept a quote. If anything is unclear, ask us before we start.

### 1. Who we are

Jane Plumbing is the trading name of Jane Smith, a sole trader, of 1 High Street, Southampton.

Phone: 07000 000001. Email: jane@example.com.

We hold public liability insurance of £5,000,000. A copy of the certificate is available on request.

### 2. Quotes and estimates

- A quote is a fixed price for the work described in it. It is valid for 30 days from the date on it.
- An estimate is our best assessment of the likely cost and is not a fixed price. We will tell you clearly whether you have been given a quote or an estimate.
- Our quote is based on what we could see and what you told us. If, once work starts, we find something that changes the work or the price, we will stop, explain it, and give you a revised price in writing. We will not carry on with the extra work until you agree to it. You can choose not to go ahead with the extra work.
- We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added to our prices.

### 3. When a contract is made

A contract is made when you accept our quote, in writing, by text, by email, or by asking us to go ahead. From that point these terms apply. We will confirm the start date, or a date range, in writing.

### 4. Your right to cancel

If you are a consumer and we agreed the job at your home, by phone, by email or online, you have the right to cancel within 14 days of the day the contract was made, without giving a reason.

To cancel, tell us clearly by phone, text, email or post using the contact details above. You can use the cancellation form at the end of these terms, but you do not have to.

If you want us to start work during those 14 days, you must ask us to in writing (a text or email is fine) and confirm you understand you will lose the right to cancel once the work is finished. If you then cancel part way through, you must pay for the work done up to the point you cancelled, in proportion to the full price. If we have finished the work within the 14 days at your request, you cannot cancel.

If you cancel and we have already bought materials made to your order that we cannot return or reuse, we may charge you their cost. We will tell you about any such materials before we order them.

Outside the 14-day period, if you cancel a booked visit with less than 24 hours' notice, we may charge the reasonable costs we have actually incurred, such as travel or materials already purchased.

### 5. Payment

- The balance is due on completion of the work, unless we have agreed something different in writing.
- We accept bank transfer and card.
- We will give you an invoice or receipt for every payment.
- Materials we supply remain our property until they are paid for in full, unless they have been permanently fixed to your property.
- If a payment is late, we may charge interest at 8% a year on the amount outstanding from the due date until it is paid. We will remind you before charging any interest.

### 6. Doing the work

- We will carry out the work with reasonable care and skill, within a reasonable time, and as described in the quote. This is your legal right under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and nothing in these terms reduces it.
- We will keep to the agreed start date where we can. If we need to change it, we will tell you as soon as possible and agree a new date.
- We may use trusted subcontractors for parts of the work. We remain responsible for the work they do.
- We will leave the work area tidy. Removal of waste is not included unless the quote says otherwise.

### 7. What we need from you

- Give us accurate information about the work and the property.
- Give us safe access to the areas we need, at the agreed times, and tell us about any hazards you know of.
- Tell us about anything hidden that we could not see, such as pipes, cables, or previous work.
- Get any permissions needed from a landlord, freeholder, managing agent or local authority before we start, unless we have agreed to do this for you.
- Pay on time.

If we cannot do the work because of something on this list, we may charge the reasonable costs of the wasted visit.

### 8. Our guarantee

We guarantee our workmanship for 24 months from the date the work is completed. If a fault appears in that time that is caused by our work, we will put it right at no cost to you.

This guarantee does not cover wear and tear, damage caused by misuse, neglect or accident, work done by someone else, or problems with parts of the property we did not work on. Materials and parts are covered by the manufacturer's guarantee, which we will pass on to you.

This guarantee is in addition to your legal rights, including your right under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 to have faulty work repeated or fixed, or to a price reduction if that is not possible.

### 9. If something goes wrong

If we cause damage to your property through our negligence, we will repair it or pay for its repair. We are responsible for losses you suffer that are a foreseeable result of our breaking this contract or failing to use reasonable care and skill.

We are not responsible for losses that were not foreseeable when the contract was made, for losses caused by something you did or failed to do, or for pre-existing defects in the property that we did not cause and could not reasonably have seen.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be excluded by law. If you are a consumer, nothing in these terms affects your legal rights.

### 10. Things outside our control

If we are delayed by something outside our reasonable control, such as severe weather, supplier delays or illness, we will tell you as soon as we can and agree a new date. We will not be liable for that delay. If the delay is substantial, you can end the contract and receive a refund for any work not done.

### 11. Photos of our work

We may take photos of the work before, during and after the job for our records and to show the quality of our work. We will only use photos on our website, Google profile or social media if you agree, and we will never show your address, house number or anything that identifies you.

### 12. Complaints

If you are unhappy with anything, tell us as soon as possible on 07000 000001 or jane@example.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days and aim to resolve it within 14 days.

If we can't resolve it, we'll tell you about an independent dispute resolution service, and whether we'll use it. You can also contact Citizens Advice for free, impartial help.

### 13. Changes to these terms

We may change these terms. Changes apply to jobs quoted after the date at the top. They do not change a contract already made.

### 14. Law and courts

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. You can bring a claim in the courts of England and Wales, or in the courts of Scotland or Northern Ireland if you live there.

### 15. Business customers

If you are buying our services for a business rather than as a consumer, clauses 4 and 9 do not apply and the following apply instead: you may not cancel after accepting a quote except by agreement; late payments carry interest and compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998; our total liability for any job is limited to the price of that job, except for death, personal injury, fraud or anything that cannot be limited by law; and we are not liable for loss of profit or indirect loss.
