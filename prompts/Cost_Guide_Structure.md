## H2s

- What Does [SERVICE_TITLE] Cost in [CITY]? | the typical range from the PRICE rows and the one thing that moves it most; factors mode: what moves the price, no figures
- What Affects the Price of [SERVICE]? | the price factors from the PRICE rows' "what moves it" and the trade facts price factor rows, each tied to the work it adds
- How to Get an Accurate Quote for [SERVICE] | what the business checks before quoting, from the confirmed practices and the quote method; what the reader can measure or note before the visit
