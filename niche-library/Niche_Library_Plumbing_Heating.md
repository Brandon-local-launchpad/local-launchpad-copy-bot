## Steps

Standard (type A):
1. **Tell Us What's Wrong.** Brief: how to get in touch and what is useful to say first: what is happening and in which room, whether the heating or the hot water is off, the make of the boiler and the fault code on its display if it shows one, and photos of the boiler, the radiator or the leak if they have them. Nothing behind the boiler's casing is ever a job for the reader. No promise of a reply time unless [CV: response_time] is set.
2. **We Look at the System and Price the Job.** (only when the `site_visit_before_quote` practice is confirmed) Brief: the visit and what gets looked at: the boiler and its controls, the pipework and the radiators, the water pressure, where the flue and the condensate pipe run, what the system water looks like, how a new boiler or cylinder would get in. Follows [CV: quote_method]; only say the quote is written if quote_method says so. Never restates "free" (FAQ 5 owns it). Gas work is done by an engineer on the Gas Safe Register as a fact of the trade, never a boast; the business's own registration only from [CV: verified_logos] and the accreditation client values.
   Without the practice (`step_2_no_visit`): **We Price Your Job.** Brief: a price is agreed before any work starts. Describe how the price is reached only from [CV: quote_method]; when quote_method is blank, name no method at all (no photos, call, video or visit). Only say the quote is written if quote_method says so. Never restates "free" (FAQ 5 owns it).
3. **We Fit or Fix It on a Date That Suits You.** Brief: the date is agreed in advance, to suit the customer. Where a job means a spell without heating or hot water (a boiler or cylinder change, from the trade facts timing cells) say so plainly and leave the length of it to the visit. Never mention the price, the quote or changes to the plan (step 2 and Why choose us own them). Never restates the start time (FAQ 4 owns it).

Emergency (`steps_emergency`, only when the emergency layout is on: the page is flagged emergency in the library and the client confirms call outs):
1. **Call Us.** Brief: what to say (what is happening, where the water is, whether the heating or hot water is off, the fault code if the boiler shows one) and what to do meanwhile, in plain words: for a leak, the stopcock shut (the trade facts: the internal stop tap turned clockwise) and people kept away from wet electrics; for a smell of gas or a carbon monoxide alarm sounding, the gas emergency line first, before this business, with the number only as the trade facts give it; for a boiler, the fault code and the pressure gauge read and nothing inside the casing touched. The number from [CV: phone]. No promise of a reply or an arrival time unless [CV: response_time] is set.
2. **We Tell You What We Can Do and When.** Brief: an arrival time only as the business gives it on the call: no window or response time written here unless [CV: response_time] is set. A call out charge only from [CV: quote_call_out_fee]; the price for making it safe agreed before anyone comes out only from the price_agreed_before_work practice and [CV: quote_method]; what the repair costs waits until the fault has been seen. Never restates "free". "Same day" only when [CV: same_day_available] is yes; evenings and weekends only when [CV: out_of_hours_available] is yes.
3. **We Make It Safe and Fix What We Can.** Brief: what usually happens on the visit, from the trade facts emergency row: the hazard found, the water or the gas isolated, the area made safe, the fault diagnosed, the repair done on the visit where the part is to hand; otherwise the system left safe and a return booked with the part. A gas fitting found unsafe is turned off or disconnected with the customer's permission and not used until it is put right (the trade facts unsafe situations rows). Never promise a same visit fix. Never mention the price.
