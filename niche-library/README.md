# Writing a niche entry

A niche entry is `niche-library/<stem>.json` (the file the code reads) and `niche-library/Niche_Library_<Niche>.md` (the same content for people, written in the same commit). The model entry is `landscaper.json` with `Niche_Library_Landscaper.md`: same slots, same depth. The schema the code checks is `niche-library/entry-schema.js`; run `node tools/niche-entry-check.js <stem>` until it reports 0 FAIL and read every WARN.

## Sources, in order of authority

1. `calibration-packs/<Niche>_Calibration_Pack_vN.md`. Section 1 gives the readers and their fears; section 2 gives the business type per GBP category and the split of services the block 4 cards follow (desire led, problem led, schedule led, and any split of its own); section 7 (what makes copy fail) feeds words to avoid and the "never" lines in briefs.
2. `trade-facts/<stem>.md`. FAQ answer briefs and step briefs point at the facts rows (Table A how each job is done, Table B what goes wrong, Table C rules, Table E facts the copy will need, Table F price factors). Never write a figure or a rule into the entry that the facts pack does not state. Where a brief needs a fact the pack lacks, the brief says "from the trade facts rows only" and names nothing.
3. `niche-library/<stem>.csv` (and `<stem>-categories.csv` where there is one). Service words come from the page targets; category work nouns from the GBP categories; core services per GBP category from its page rows. `fillout/services/<niche>.json` gives the GBP category names a client's tabs carry, which are the keys `category_types`, `category_work_nouns` and `core_services` are looked up by (lower case).

## Rules

- Business type per category follows the pack's section 2. `business_type_default` is the type of the primary category; `category_types` lists only the categories that differ.
- Emergency is per page through the library's `emergency` column. A niche with flagged pages gets an `emergency` variant inside its default type's entry in `category_page_blocks` and `service_page_blocks` (block 4 as "when this can't wait", `steps`, and `faq_slots` that answer the urgent reader), plus `steps_emergency` and `buttons.final_h2_emergency` at the top level. The old `emergency_niche` marker stays as it is; nothing reads it.
- No new business types in code. Where the pack uses a split the schema has no slot for (obligation led, constraint cards, specification led, need led, skill led), write it into the card briefs under the existing type: the brief says which services on the page follow which framing.
- Words to use are the customer's words, not trade jargon. Words to avoid are the trade's own jargon, the pack's section 7 failures and anything the pack bans; `words_avoid_exceptions` lists a banned word that a page name needs.
- A block 4 card has a title, a `mechanism` (its one cause, a few words) and a brief that says the symptom or situation, the one cause, what the fix usually involves in plain words, what the business itself does only from confirmed practices, and what the card must not say. Block 5 cards say what moves the price and never give a figure. Step briefs follow `[CV: quote_method]` and the confirmed practices. FAQ slots name the slot (cost, cover, scope, process, timing for type A; frequency, cost, access, scope, timing for type B; a location page adds `town`), a `when` where the question only holds with a client value (guarantee, insurance, price_list, start_time, quote_free, not_quote_free, rule_rows), the question with placeholders, an answer brief and the doubt it answers (Price, Trust, Fit, Process, Timing).
- Placeholders: `[CAT]`, `[CAT_LOWER]`, `[WORK]`, `[WORK_LOWER]`, `[CITY]`, `[TOWN]`, `[SERVICE]`, `[SERVICE_LOWER]`, `[SYMPTOM]`, `[QUOTE_FREE]`, `[QUOTE_FREE_LOWER]`, `[CV: key]`. Nothing else in square brackets.
- No em or en dashes anywhere in the entry. No claims (free, same day, 24/7, fixed price, prices, materials, guarantees) in a brief except as "from [CV: key] only".
- `service_words` is the old path's list (a homepage written from uploaded custom values CSVs): `["text the GBP name contains", "how a person says it" or null]`; a client with a v3 page map names its services from the page map instead, so the list need not cover every page target. `general_service_words` are the plain words a FAQ about the trade uses. `service_terms` are the trade's own terms the validator treats as the trade's words.
- `materials_tick_list` groups the materials and methods the onboarding form asks the client to tick for this trade; the copy may only name a material the client ticked. `logo_tiers` lists the rating sites and the trade bodies from the facts pack's Table D, best first. `fears` are the four things this trade's customer is afraid of, from the pack's section 1. `why_titles_alt` are two alternative titles for the homepage's section 5. `cover_chain` is the order the cover FAQ tries: guarantee, manufacturer_warranty, insurance, as the trade warrants.
- The `.md` mirror has the same sections as `Niche_Library_Landscaper.md`, in the same order, with the briefs as tables. Every block 4 card title in the `.json` appears in the `.md`.

## A new niche

A new niche is a new entry, not code: `server.js` finds the file from the client's trade through the niche key and the library stem (`ui/library.js stemFor`), the page jobs read the briefs by business type, and the Niche library screen shows the entry and its briefs. Test it with `node tools/niche-thin-run.js <stem>` (a thin client of that niche: one homepage, one category page and one service page with the real model) before it is used on a client.
