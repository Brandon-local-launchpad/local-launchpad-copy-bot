## Steps (type A)

Each step has a fixed title and a one line brief the model writes to.

1. **Tell Us What's Happening**
   Brief: How to get in touch and what is useful to say first: what is slow, blocked or backing up and where; whether it is one sink or toilet or several at once (the trade facts: one fixture points at its own trap and waste, several outlets or the outside chamber point at the shared run); whether the outside chamber is full or overflowing, seen from above without lifting anything; whether next door has the same; how long it has been going on; a photo of the chamber or the wet ground if one can be taken safely. Nothing down the drain meanwhile: no chemical cleaner (the trade facts: caustic cleaners injure, and the engineer meets what was poured). No promise of a reply time unless [CV: response_time] is set.
2. **We Find the Cause and Price the Work** (only when the `site_visit_before_quote` practice is confirmed)
   Brief: The visit and what gets looked at: which run is affected and where its access is, whether the pipe is the customer's own or a shared one the water company looks after (the trade facts: ownership identified before the work), what the camera shows where a fault is suspected (the trade facts stages: the run cleared, then inspected if a defect is suspected). For a survey the price is for the survey; a repair, a patch repair or a liner is priced off the footage, never before it (the pack's craft rule). Follows [CV: quote_method]; only say the quote is written if quote_method says so. Never restates 'free' (FAQ 5 owns it).
   Without the visit practice (`step_2_no_visit`): **We Price Your Job**
   Brief: Say a price is agreed before any work starts. Describe how the price is reached only from [CV: quote_method]; when quote_method is blank, name no method at all (no photos, call, video or visit). For a survey the price is for the survey, and any repair is priced off the footage. Only say the quote is written if quote_method says so. Never restates 'free' (FAQ 5 owns it).
3. **We Do the Work on a Date That Suits You**
   Brief: The date is agreed in advance, to suit the customer. For a survey, what the customer is given at the end (the footage, a plan of the runs, a written report) only from the trade facts stages for that service and the confirmed practices. Never mention the price, the quote or changes to the plan (step 2 and Why choose us own them). Never restates the start time (FAQ 4 owns it).

Emergency (only when the emergency layout is on: the page is flagged emergency in the library and the client confirms call outs), `steps_emergency`:

1. **Call Us**
   Brief: What to say: what is backing up and where, whether it is one fixture or several, whether the outside chamber is full or overflowing, whether next door has it too, how long it has been going. What to do meanwhile, from the trade facts rows only: keep out of the sewage water and keep cuts covered (the trade facts: contact with contaminated water carries infection), nothing poured down the drain (chemical cleaners injure and stay in the pipe for the engineer), nobody leaning into or climbing down a chamber (the trade facts: the first control is not entering). Where more than one house is affected or the blockage is past the boundary, the water company is called first (the trade facts who to call first row). The number from [CV: phone]. No promise of a reply or an arrival time unless [CV: response_time] is set.
2. **We Find Where It's Blocked and Whose It Is**
   Brief: What usually happens first on an emergency visit, from the trade facts emergency drainage stages: the flooding and contamination looked at, whether the pipe is private or the water company's established, the area isolated, then the blockage contained or cleared. The customer told what was found. A call out charge only from [CV: quote_call_out_fee]; the price agreed before any work starts only from the price_agreed_before_work practice and [CV: quote_method]. Never restates 'free'.
3. **We Clear It and Tell You What We Found**
   Brief: The blockage cleared and the flow confirmed on the visit where the access and the cause allow; where the camera shows a fault behind it, the fault recorded and a repair agreed for another day (the trade facts: any defect needing later repair is recorded; clearing without investigating a structural fault lets it recur). A survey offered as the next step, never as a sales pitch, and never a promise the blockage will not come back. Never promise a same visit fix; 'same day' only when [CV: same_day_available] is yes. Never mention the price.

The category and service page emergency variants carry the same three steps (below).
