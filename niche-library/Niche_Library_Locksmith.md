## Steps

Standard (type A, every planned page):

1. **Tell Us About the Door.** Brief: How to get in touch and what is useful to send first: what the door is (timber, uPVC or composite), what the lock is doing or what they want (a change after moving in, a lock the policy asks for, a stiffening handle), a photo of the lock from inside and outside if they have one, how many doors or windows. No promise of a reply time unless [CV: response_time] is set. Never an instruction to take a lock or a handle apart.
2. **We Look at the Lock and Price the Job.** (only when the `site_visit_before_quote` practice is confirmed) Brief: The visit and what gets looked at: the door and how it is built, the lock on it and whether it still works with the door open and shut, what the policy or the escape route needs (the Table A stages for lock fitting and the British Standard page). Follows [CV: quote_method]; only say the quote is written if quote_method says so. Never restates 'free' (FAQ 5 owns it). Never 'insurance approved' as a lock's status.
   Without the practice (`step_2_no_visit`): **We Price Your Job.** Brief: Say a price is agreed before any work starts. Describe how the price is reached only from [CV: quote_method]; when quote_method is blank, name no method at all (no photos, call, video or visit). Only say the quote is written if quote_method says so. Never restates 'free' (FAQ 5 owns it).
3. **We Fit It and Hand Over the Keys.** Brief: The date is agreed in advance, to suit the customer. What the customer gets at the end: the lock tested from both sides, every key tried, the keys handed over and, on a British Standard lock, its identification recorded for them (Table A). The old lock taken away only from the waste_removal practice. Never mention the price, the quote or changes to the plan (step 2 and Why choose us own them). Never restates the start time (FAQ 4 owns it).

Emergency (`steps_emergency`, only when the emergency layout is on: the page is flagged emergency in the library and the client confirms call outs):

1. **Call Us.** Brief: What to say: where they are, what the door is (timber with a key lock, uPVC or composite with a handle), what has happened (shut on the latch with the keys inside, the key snapped, the lock will not turn, a break in), whether anyone is inside. What to have ready for when the locksmith arrives: something that shows they belong at that address, since the Table E lockout authority row has the trade asking (a licence with the address, other identification, a neighbour who can vouch; a business sends an authority letter). What to do meanwhile: leave the key and the door alone rather than force either, and keep clear of broken glass after a break in. Never a word on how the door will be opened. The number from [CV: phone]. No promise of a reply or an arrival time unless [CV: response_time] is set.
2. **We Agree the Price on the Phone.** Brief: The price settled before anyone drives out only from the price_agreed_before_work practice and [CV: quote_method]; any call out charge or out of hours rate only from [CV: quote_call_out_fee] and [CV: price_list], and never 'no call out charge' unless quote_call_out_fee says there is none (Table E: the claim misleads where a compulsory charge exists under any name). What the price covers and what would change it (a lock that has failed rather than a key left inside, parts needed: Table F lockouts) said as what is explained on the call. An arrival time only as the business gives it on the call; no window written here unless [CV: response_time] is set. 'Same day' only when [CV: same_day_available] is yes; round the clock cover only when [CV: emergency_callouts] or [CV: out_of_hours_available] gives it. Never restates 'free'.
3. **We Get You In and Leave the Door Secure.** Brief: What usually happens on arrival, from the Table A lockouts and emergency rows: the right to be there checked, the lock and the door looked at, the way in that leaves the least mark chosen where the lock allows it, with drilling the last option and a replacement lock after it; then whatever failed found and put right or replaced if authorised, the lock tested and the keys handed over. Written as how the work is approached, never as a promise the door comes to no harm (Table E: entry without damage is an option, not a guarantee). Nothing on how entry is made. Never promise a same visit replacement where a part is obsolete. Never mention the price.
