- Building Regulations for lofts: Part B (the protected stairway and fire doors FD30 to every habitable room off the escape route in a three storey house, the escape window alternative only in two storey, mains interlinked smoke alarms, the 30 minute floor), Part A (floor joists independent of the ceiling joists, the steel beams and padstones), Part K (stair pitch 42°, the 2 m headroom and the 1.9 m / 1.8 m reduced headroom allowance for loft stairs, handrails), Part L (insulation in the roof and dormer, the U values 0.15 and 0.18, the ventilation gap above insulation), Part F, Part E (sound between the loft and neighbours), Part P, the completion certificate and why lenders require it, the Party Wall Act for terraced and semi detached lofts (the steel beams bearing on the party wall).

- Bats and birds: the Habitats Regulations, the bat survey trigger (any roof work on an older house with signs), the nesting season.
